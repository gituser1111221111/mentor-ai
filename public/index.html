Common questions.

What does the agent do, and what does the mentor do?

The agent builds options using tools and your context. The mentor applies a financial philosophy to select an option and explain it.

Can I talk instead of type?

Voice is available when the service is configured. You can also type your questions and follow-ups in the same conversation.

What can I try for free?

Chat with the Ramsey-, Buffett-, or Wood-inspired mentors for free. Ramsey also supports voice. The money quiz is optional; additional Plus features are marked as planned.

How are the numbers checked?

Budget calculations run in code. Proposed options are checked against those calculations before the mentor selects one.

What if my income changes every month?

The plan uses your lowest reported month to check whether your essentials are covered. The calculation is available in chat.

Can I switch mentors?

Yes. Ramsey, Buffett, and Wood are available now. Buffett and Wood offer text mentorship with distinct research perspectives; live voice is available with Ramsey. Other mentors remain planned.

Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. You can enter your numbers yourself. A portfolio URL is saved as a reference only; a holdings CSV can be summarized in this demo.
