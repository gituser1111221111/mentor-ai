Common questions.

What if my income changes every month?

The plan uses your lowest reported month to check whether your essentials are covered. You can review the calculation before starting coaching.

Can I switch mentors?

Yes. A different mentor can choose from the same verified options, so the facts and calculations stay the same.

Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. You can enter your numbers yourself. A portfolio URL is saved as a reference only; a holdings CSV can be summarized in this demo.
