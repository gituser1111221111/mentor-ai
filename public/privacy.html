Privacy Policy

Placeholder text written for a hackathon prototype. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before any real launch.

What we collect

The numbers you enter in the six-question intake (income by month, bills, spending, debts, savings, goals), an optional portfolio CSV summary, text extracted from optional financial documents, and your chat messages. A portfolio URL saved in the context hub stays in browser session storage and does not connect to your brokerage. We do not ask for your bank login or brokerage password. Please remove account numbers before uploading.

How it's stored

Your data is tied to an anonymous id stored in your browser. In this prototype, the session, including extracted document text, is saved in a temporary server file without encryption at rest. It may persist until the hosting instance is replaced or you use "Start over" to clear your session. Do not upload documents you are uncomfortable sharing with this prototype.

Voice conversations

When you enable Talk live and allow microphone access, recorded speech is sent through our server to ElevenLabs for transcription. The transcript is sent to Claude as a chat message. Reply text is sent to ElevenLabs to generate synthetic audio. Recording resumes between replies while live mode is active; End call stops the conversation. Typed messages do not automatically play audio.

Who processes it

Your questions, numbers, and relevant extracted document text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate options and mentor replies. This demo does not collect payment information or enroll you in a paid subscription. Hosting is provided by Render. ElevenLabs processes voice audio and speech text. These providers process data under their own terms and retention policies; clearing your app session does not necessarily remove provider logs.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Contact

A public privacy contact and a verified data-rights process must be established before launch. Do not post personal information or financial documents in public issues.
