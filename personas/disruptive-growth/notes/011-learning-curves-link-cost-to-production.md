# Learning curves link cost to production
source: ARK explanation of Wright's Law, What is Wright's Law; comparison with Moore's Law. https://www.ark-invest.com/wrights-law

ARK uses cumulative production to reason about potential cost declines. This differs from assuming cost changes merely because time passes.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Cost curves are conditional models, not physical guarantees for every technology.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
