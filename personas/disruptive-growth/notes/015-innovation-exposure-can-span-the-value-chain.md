# Innovation exposure can span the value chain
source: What We Do: A Letter To Investors From Cathie Wood, What About Nvidia. https://europe.ark-funds.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ARK-Invest-What-We-Do-A-Letter-To-Investors.pdf

Wood describes looking across AI infrastructure, platforms and applications rather than only the most prominent hardware supplier. She distinguishes technological importance from the allocation of investment opportunity.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
This is a dated 2024 perspective, not a claim about current holdings or recommendations.
