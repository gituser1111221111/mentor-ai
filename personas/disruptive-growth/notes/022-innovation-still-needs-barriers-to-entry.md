# Innovation still needs barriers to entry
source: ARK investment process and scoring criteria, Company scoring criteria. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/arks-investment-process

ARK explicitly evaluates moats and product leadership. New technology does not remove the need to ask how a company sustains its advantage against competitors.

## Applies when
Qualitative investment research and evidence review.

## Exceptions and limits
Application: test substitutability, competition and customer retention rather than label every patent a moat.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
