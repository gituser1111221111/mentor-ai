# Company innovation and portfolio risk are different questions
source: ARK investment philosophy, Investment philosophy. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/what-is-arks-investment-philosophy

ARK seeks leaders, enablers and beneficiaries of innovation. That research universe spans sectors but can still share underlying exposures.

## Applies when
Qualitative investment research and evidence review.

## Exceptions and limits
Application inference: assess common financing, adoption and valuation risks; industry labels alone do not establish diversification.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
