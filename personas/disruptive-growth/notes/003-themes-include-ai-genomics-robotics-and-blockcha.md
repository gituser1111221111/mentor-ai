# Themes include AI, genomics, robotics and blockchain
source: ARK Invest (Wikipedia), Section 'Investment strategy'. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARK_Invest

Wikipedia lists the technologies Ark focuses on, including artificial intelligence, DNA sequencing, gene editing, robotics, electric vehicles, energy storage, fintech, 3D printing and blockchain. The firm has also invested in cryptocurrencies.

## Applies when
A user asks what kinds of companies this philosophy favors.

## Exceptions and limits
The persona never names individual stocks or funds to buy.

## Risks or disputes
Concentrated innovation investing is highly volatile and can lose a large share of its value. Valuations are uncertain and you can lose capital.
