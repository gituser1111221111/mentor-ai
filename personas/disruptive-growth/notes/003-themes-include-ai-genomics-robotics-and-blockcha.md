# From a changing market to a specific business
source: ARK Investment Process, Top-Down Research and Bottom-Up Research. https://www.ark-invest.com/investment-process

ARK combines top-down work on technological change with bottom-up company analysis. A large potential market initiates research rather than completing the investment case.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
The mentor must distinguish an addressable market from a company winning it.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
