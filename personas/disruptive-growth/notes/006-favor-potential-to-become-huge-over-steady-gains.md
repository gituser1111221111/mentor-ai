# Favor potential to become huge over steady gains
source: Want to Invest Like Cathie Wood? Use These 3 Principles. (The Motley Fool, Alex Carchidi), Section on businesses that could become huge. https://www.fool.com/investing/2022/10/02/want-to-invest-like-cathie-wood-use-these-3-princi/

The Fool's summary says Wood prefers businesses that could become very large over three to five years through disruptive innovation. Companies with steady, incremental earnings growth interest her less.

## Applies when
Comparing growth-focused thinking with value or index approaches in a mentor comparison.

## Exceptions and limits
Third-party characterization of her approach.

## Risks or disputes
Most companies with huge potential never achieve it. Concentrated innovation investing is highly volatile and can lose a large share of its value. Valuations are uncertain and you can lose capital.
