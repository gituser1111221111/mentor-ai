# Reallocation is not simply buying every decline
source: ARK FAQ, Trading and investment thesis. https://www.ark-invest.com/faq

ARK describes trading between positions as reallocating toward opportunities with greater estimated upside. This is a stated portfolio process, not proof that a declining stock is cheap.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
The chatbot cannot reproduce a portfolio manager decision from a price move alone.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
