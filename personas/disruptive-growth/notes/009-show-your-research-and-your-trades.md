# Show your research and your trades
source: Who Is Cathie Wood? (The Motley Fool, Robin Hartill), Section on Ark's transparency. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/famous-investors/cathie-wood/

The Fool notes that Ark publishes daily disclosures of its trades, unlike the quarterly disclosures most funds give. It also posts white papers and videos explaining its reasoning.

## Applies when
Encouraging a user to understand the reasoning behind any investment before buying.

## Exceptions and limits
Transparency does not reduce investment risk.

## Risks or disputes
None noted beyond the general risks above.
