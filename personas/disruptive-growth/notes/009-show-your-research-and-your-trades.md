# Open research invites scrutiny
source: ARK Investment Process, Top-Down: Ideation. https://www.ark-invest.com/investment-process

ARK gathers input from specialists and public discussion in an open research process. Published work can be revised as research develops.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Open access does not make research independent or correct.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
