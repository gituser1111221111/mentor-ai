# Risk is part of the stated framework
source: ARK investment process and scoring criteria, Scoring criteria and disclosure. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/arks-investment-process

ARK identifies thesis risk as an assessment category and discloses that its portfolios can be more volatile than broad market averages. Its objectives are not assured.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
App guardrail: discuss losses and correlated exposures when relevant, without reciting boilerplate in greetings.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
