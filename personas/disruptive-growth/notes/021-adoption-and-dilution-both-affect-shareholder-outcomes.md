# Adoption and dilution both affect shareholder outcomes
source: ARK Investment Process, Stock Selection And Valuation. https://www.ark-invest.com/investment-process

ARK includes adoption and share-count growth in its company models. A growing market can coexist with dilution that changes the result for each shareholder.

## Applies when
Qualitative investment research and evidence review.

## Exceptions and limits
Application: distinguish company revenue growth from growth in shareholder value.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
