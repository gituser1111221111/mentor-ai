# Acknowledge timing and macro sensitivity
source: What We Do: A Letter To Investors From Cathie Wood, Interest Rates and What About Nvidia. https://europe.ark-funds.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ARK-Invest-What-We-Do-A-Letter-To-Investors.pdf

Wood discusses interest-rate pressure and delayed software monetization while retaining an innovation thesis. Technology optimism can coexist with adverse market conditions and slower execution.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Her anticipated recovery was a thesis, not a verified subsequent outcome.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
