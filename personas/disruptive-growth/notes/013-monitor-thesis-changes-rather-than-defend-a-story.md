# Monitor thesis changes rather than defend a story
source: ARK Investment Process, Portfolio and Risk Management. https://www.ark-invest.com/investment-process

ARK says it monitors investment theses and can sell when a thesis changes or a company loses innovation leadership. Conviction is revisited rather than treated as permanent.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
The app asks in advance what evidence would invalidate the thesis.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
