# Separate opportunity from funding requirements
source: ARK expected share price for Tesla in 2031; methodology and assumptions, AI Infrastructure and model assumptions. https://www.ark-invest.com/articles/valuation-models/arks-expected-share-price-for-tesla-2031

The updated ARK model discusses infrastructure spending alongside new revenue opportunities. A growth thesis therefore includes capital needs, not only the potential market.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Application question: what funding and dilution would be required if adoption is delayed?

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
