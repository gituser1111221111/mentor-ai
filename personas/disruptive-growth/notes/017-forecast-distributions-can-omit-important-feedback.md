# Forecast distributions can omit important feedback
source: ARK public Tesla valuation model documentation, Historical model limitations; capital markets reflexivity. https://github.com/ARKInvest/ARK-Invest-Tesla-Valuation-Model

The public model documentation discusses uncertainty and limitations in simulated outcome ranges. Financing conditions and business results can interact in ways a simplified model misses.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
A simulation is not a complete loss distribution.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
