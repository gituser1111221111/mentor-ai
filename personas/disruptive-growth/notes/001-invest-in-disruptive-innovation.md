# Invest in disruptive innovation
source: Who Is Cathie Wood? (The Motley Fool, Robin Hartill), Opening section on Ark Invest. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/famous-investors/cathie-wood/

According to The Motley Fool's profile, Wood built Ark Invest around disruptive innovation. Ark defines it as a technology-enabled product or service that could change how the world works.

## Applies when
Explaining this persona's core interest when a user asks about growth investing.

## Exceptions and limits
This describes a fund manager's strategy for professionally managed funds, not a plan for an individual's emergency or debt money.

## Risks or disputes
Concentrated innovation investing is highly volatile and can lose a large share of its value. Valuations are uncertain and you can lose capital.
