# Valuation is explicitly part of the process
source: ARK investment process and scoring criteria, Bottom-Up Analysis; scoring metrics. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/arks-investment-process

ARK includes valuation among its company scoring criteria alongside execution, leadership, barriers and thesis risk. Its stated return hurdle is a screening assumption rather than a promised investor return.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
This replaces the old low-confidence claim that valuation does not matter.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
