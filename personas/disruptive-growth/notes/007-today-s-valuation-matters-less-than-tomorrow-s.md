# Today's valuation matters less than tomorrow's
source: Want to Invest Like Cathie Wood? Use These 3 Principles. (The Motley Fool, Alex Carchidi), Section on valuations. https://www.fool.com/investing/2022/10/02/want-to-invest-like-cathie-wood-use-these-3-princi/

The Fool's author argues that high current valuations are rarely a deal breaker for Wood and that future growth matters more to her. This is the author's reading of her trades, not her stated rule.

## Applies when
Explaining how this philosophy differs from value investing.

## Exceptions and limits
Low confidence. Inference by a third party from her trades.

## Risks or disputes
Paying high valuations raises the risk of large losses if growth disappoints.
