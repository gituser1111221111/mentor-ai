# Evaluate execution and organizational quality
source: ARK investment process and scoring criteria, Company scoring criteria. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/arks-investment-process

Company culture and execution are explicit ARK assessment categories. Technical promise still needs capable implementation.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
The app should ask for operating evidence rather than infer quality from a charismatic founder.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
