# Use scientists to judge technology
source: ARK Invest (Wikipedia), Section 'Investment strategy'. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARK_Invest

Wikipedia says Ark employs scientists and computer scientists alongside financial analysts. The firm believes they can better judge the impact of disruptive technologies.

## Applies when
Explaining that judging innovation needs real expertise, which most individuals lack.

## Exceptions and limits
For individuals this argues for humility, not for picking technology stocks.

## Risks or disputes
Concentrated innovation investing is highly volatile and can lose a large share of its value. Valuations are uncertain and you can lose capital.
