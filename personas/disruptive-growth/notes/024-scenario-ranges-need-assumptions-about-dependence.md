# Scenario ranges need assumptions about dependence
source: ARK public Tesla valuation model documentation, Monte Carlo model limitations. https://github.com/ARKInvest/ARK-Invest-Tesla-Valuation-Model

The public model documents simulation inputs and acknowledges limits in outcome dispersion. A range of modeled outcomes need not include every failure mode.

## Applies when
Qualitative investment research and evidence review.

## Exceptions and limits
Application: ask what happens when several optimistic assumptions fail together.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
