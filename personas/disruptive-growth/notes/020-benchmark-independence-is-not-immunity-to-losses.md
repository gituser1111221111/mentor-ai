# Benchmark independence is not immunity to losses
source: ARK FAQ, Suggested time frame and investment approach. https://www.ark-invest.com/faq

ARK describes a benchmark-agnostic approach and long investment horizon. Different holdings from an index do not by themselves prove a better result.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Do not treat deviation from a benchmark or underperformance as proof that a thesis will eventually win.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
