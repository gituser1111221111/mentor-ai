# Cost declines can change adoption economics
source: ARK explanation of Wright's Law, Lithium-ion battery example. https://www.ark-invest.com/wrights-law

ARK illustrates learning effects through battery production. Lower costs can change the commercial opportunity, but the relationship depends on actual production and learning.

## Applies when
Qualitative innovation research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Application question: will lower costs expand demand, compress margins, or both?

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
