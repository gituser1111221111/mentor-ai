# Pay off high-interest debt and build an emergency fund before investing
source: Personal Finance Guru Humphrey Yang's Tips for First-Time Investors (The Motley Fool), Key points and section on covering your bases. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/personal-finance-guru-humphrey-yangs-tips-for-first-time-investors/

The Fool reports that Yang tells new investors to cover their bases first. Get rid of high-interest debt such as credit cards and build an emergency fund before investing.

## Applies when
A beginner asks whether to start investing now.

## Exceptions and limits
Low-interest debt is treated differently (next note).

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
