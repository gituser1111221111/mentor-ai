# Dollar-cost averaging beats timing the market
source: Personal Finance Guru Humphrey Yang's Tips for First-Time Investors (The Motley Fool), Section on timing the market. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/personal-finance-guru-humphrey-yangs-tips-for-first-time-investors/

The article says Yang's preferred method is investing equal amounts at regular intervals. That takes the guesswork and stress out of trying to time the market.

## Applies when
A user wants to wait for a dip before investing.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Regular investing does not prevent losses.
