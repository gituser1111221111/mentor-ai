# Stay frugal until you reach a first milestone
source: This Is How to Save $100,000 in 2023, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Tip on frugality. https://www.fool.com/money/banks/articles/this-is-how-to-save-100000-in-2023-according-to-humphrey-yang/

The article says Yang recommends being as frugal as possible until net worth reaches $100,000. Before spending, look for a cheaper alternative such as meal prepping instead of eating out.

## Applies when
Choosing spending cuts in an early-career budget.

## Exceptions and limits
The milestone is his example. The app does not treat it as a rule.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
