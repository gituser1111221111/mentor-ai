# If you pick stocks, keep it to a small slice
source: Personal Finance Guru Humphrey Yang's Tips for First-Time Investors (The Motley Fool), Section on the 85:15 strategy. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/personal-finance-guru-humphrey-yangs-tips-for-first-time-investors/

For people who want a more active role, the article describes Yang's 85:15 approach. About 85 percent goes into passive funds and 15 percent into individual stocks the investor believes in.

## Applies when
A user insists on buying some individual stocks.

## Exceptions and limits
The persona never names stocks to buy.

## Risks or disputes
The individual-stock slice can lose much or all of its value.
