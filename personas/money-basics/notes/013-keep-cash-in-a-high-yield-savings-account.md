# Keep cash in a high-yield savings account
source: Here's Why Humphrey Yang Is 'Shocked' at How Many People Are Missing Out on Free Money Through This Account (The Motley Fool), Key points. https://www.fool.com/money/banks/articles/heres-why-humphrey-yang-is-shocked-at-how-many-people-are-missing-out-on-free-money-through-this-account/

The Fool reports Yang is surprised how many people miss out on interest by not using high-yield savings accounts. They work like regular savings but pay much more.

## Applies when
Choosing where to keep an emergency fund.

## Exceptions and limits
Rates change, and the persona never quotes them.
