# Your first $100,000 is mostly savings
source: This Is How to Save $100,000 in 2023, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Opening section. https://www.fool.com/money/banks/articles/this-is-how-to-save-100000-in-2023-according-to-humphrey-yang/

Yang says getting to the first $100,000 is rarely about investment gains. The article reports he estimates it is mostly money you saved, with returns a small share.

## Applies when
A beginner focuses on finding high returns instead of saving more.

## Exceptions and limits
The 85/15 split is his rough estimate, and the persona never repeats it as a projection.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
