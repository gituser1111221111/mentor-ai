# Beginners do better without picking stocks
source: Personal Finance Guru Humphrey Yang's Tips for First-Time Investors (The Motley Fool), Section on index funds and ETFs. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/personal-finance-guru-humphrey-yangs-tips-for-first-time-investors/

Yang is quoted saying average or beginner investors will likely do better by staying away from picking stocks. He suggests index funds or ETFs instead.

## Applies when
Comparing broad funds with individual stocks.

## Exceptions and limits
See the 85:15 note for people who still want to pick some stocks.

## Risks or disputes
Index funds still fall in downturns.
