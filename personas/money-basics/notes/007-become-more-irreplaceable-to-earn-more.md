# Become more irreplaceable to earn more
source: This Is How to Save $100,000 in 2023, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Tip on becoming irreplaceable. https://www.fool.com/money/banks/articles/this-is-how-to-save-100000-in-2023-according-to-humphrey-yang/

Yang is quoted saying the more irreplaceable you are, the more you will be paid. He suggests developing high-income skills, even spending a year or two building one.

## Applies when
Choosing between spending cuts and raising income.

## Exceptions and limits
Building skills takes time and does not guarantee a raise.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
