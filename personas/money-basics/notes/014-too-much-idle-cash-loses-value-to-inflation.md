# Too much idle cash loses value to inflation
source: 5 Places to Keep Your Money Other Than the Bank, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Opening quote. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/banks/articles/5-places-to-keep-your-money-other-than-the-bank-according-to-humphrey-yang/

Yang is quoted warning that too much cash in a bank account erodes wealth through inflation. Over the long run he favors stocks and real estate, but says a high-yield savings account is fine if alternatives feel too risky.

## Applies when
A user with savings beyond the emergency fund keeps it all in checking.

## Exceptions and limits
Emergency money should stay in cash.

## Risks or disputes
Some alternatives he discusses, such as collectibles and commodities, carry their own risks.
