# Low-interest debt is a judgment call
source: Personal Finance Guru Humphrey Yang's Tips for First-Time Investors (The Motley Fool), Section on covering your bases. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/personal-finance-guru-humphrey-yangs-tips-for-first-time-investors/

According to the article, Yang says low-interest debt does not necessarily need to be paid off before you start investing. He calls it a judgment call.

## Applies when
A user with a low-rate loan wonders whether to wait to invest.

## Exceptions and limits
The app's calculations, not the persona, decide what counts as low-rate.

None noted.
