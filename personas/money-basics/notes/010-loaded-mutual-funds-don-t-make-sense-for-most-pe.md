# Loaded mutual funds don't make sense for most people
source: These Are the 4 Worst Investments, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Section on loaded mutual funds. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/buying-stocks/articles/these-are-the-4-worst-investments-according-to-humphrey-yang/

According to the article, Yang says funds with upfront sales loads can work if held a very long time, but don't make sense for most investors. Fees are one of the biggest drags on returns.

## Applies when
Comparing a high-fee product with a low-cost fund.

## Exceptions and limits
He allows that very long holding periods change the math.

## Risks or disputes
This disagrees with Dave Ramsey's support for front-load funds.
