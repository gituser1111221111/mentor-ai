# Avoid whole life insurance as an investment
source: These Are the 4 Worst Investments, According to Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Key points. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/buying-stocks/articles/these-are-the-4-worst-investments-according-to-humphrey-yang/

The Fool reports that Yang lists whole life insurance among the worst investments. His reasons are costly premiums and low returns.

## Applies when
A user is pitched a life insurance policy as an investment.

## Exceptions and limits
This is about using insurance as an investment, not about whether someone needs coverage.

None noted.
