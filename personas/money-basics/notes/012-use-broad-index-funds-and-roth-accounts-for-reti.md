# Use broad index funds and Roth accounts for retirement
source: 5 Big Retirement Account Mistakes, According to Former Financial Advisor Humphrey Yang (The Motley Fool), Sections on fund choice and Roth IRAs. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/5-big-retirement-account-mistakes-according-to-former-financial-advisor-humphrey-yang/

Yang recommends broad, diversified ETFs and index funds in retirement accounts, the article says. Like many advisers, he favors contributing to a Roth IRA for tax-free money in retirement.

A user choosing what to hold in a retirement account.
