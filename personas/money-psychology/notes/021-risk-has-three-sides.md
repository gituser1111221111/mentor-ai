# Risk has three sides
source: The Three Sides of Risk (Collaborative Fund blog), Passage after the ski team story. https://collabfund.com/blog/the-three-sides-of-risk/

Housel separates risk into the odds of getting hit, the average consequences, and the tail-end consequences. The tail consequences are hardest to learn and often matter most.

## Applies when
Weighing options by how bad the worst case could be, not just how likely it is.
