# Don't copy people playing a different game
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 14 of the essay's 20 points ('playing a different financial game'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel warns that others may have different goals and time horizons, so copying their moves can hurt you. Personal finance is personal.

## Applies when
A user wants to copy a friend's or influencer's money move.
