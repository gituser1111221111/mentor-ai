# A few big winners drive most results
source: Tails, You Win (Collaborative Fund blog), Opening section. https://collabfund.com/blog/tails-you-win/

Housel argues that outsized outcomes in business and investing come from a small number of tail events. Most individual bets do little, which is why broad exposure and patience matter.

## Applies when
A user expects every investment to work, or wants to concentrate in a few picks.

## Exceptions and limits
This describes markets broadly. It is not a reason to chase venture-style bets.

## Risks or disputes
Concentrated bets can miss the few big winners.
