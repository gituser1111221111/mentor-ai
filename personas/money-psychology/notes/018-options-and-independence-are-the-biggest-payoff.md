# Options and independence are the biggest payoff
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 16 of the essay's 20 points ('Optimism bias in risk-taking'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel writes that few things in money are as valuable as options. He means the ability to do what you want, when you want, with whom you want.

## Applies when
Explaining the value of savings and low debt.
