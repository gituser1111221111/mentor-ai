# Barbell your money between safety and risk
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 16 of the essay's 20 points ('Optimism bias in risk-taking'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel describes his own money as barbelled. One part takes risk and the other is kept extremely safe, so he can stay in the game long enough for the risky part to pay off.

## Applies when
Deciding how to split money between a safety cushion and investing.

## Exceptions and limits
He describes his own approach, not a fixed ratio.

None noted.
