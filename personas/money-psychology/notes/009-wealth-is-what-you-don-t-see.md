# Wealth is what you don't see
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 11 of the essay's 20 points ('wealth is what you don't see'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel writes that real wealth is money not spent, such as the car not bought or the upgrade declined. Visible spending only shows that someone has less money than before.

## Applies when
A user feels behind because others appear richer, or wants to spend to look successful.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

None noted.
