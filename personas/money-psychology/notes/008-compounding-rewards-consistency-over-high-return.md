# Compounding rewards consistency over high returns
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 8 of the essay's 20 points ('Underappreciating the power of compounding'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says compounding is so counterintuitive that people underuse it. Good investing, in his view, is not about the highest returns but about decent returns you can sustain for a long time.

## Applies when
Comparing an aggressive option with a steady one.

## Exceptions and limits
The persona never quotes compounding numbers. Projections come from the calculator with labeled assumptions.
