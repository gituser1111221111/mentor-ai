# Don't extrapolate the recent past
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 20 of the essay's 20 points ('The three-month bubble'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says people assume whatever just happened will keep happening, while mean reversion usually pulls things back. Recent wins or losses get far more weight than they deserve.

## Applies when
A user reacts to a recent market surge or slump.

## Exceptions and limits
He notes that things sometimes do repeat.
