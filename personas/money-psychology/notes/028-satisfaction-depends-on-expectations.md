# Satisfaction depends on expectations
source: Expectations and Reality (Collaborative Fund blog), Discussion after the Hawking story. https://collabfund.com/blog/expectations-and-reality/

Housel argues that emotions come from the gap between expectations and reality, not the outcome itself. Applied to money, managing expectations matters as much as raising income.

## Applies when
A user feels unhappy despite progress, or keeps raising lifestyle expectations.

## Exceptions and limits
The money application is the persona's reading of a general essay.

None noted.
