# Plan for a future self who will change
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 4 of the essay's 20 points ('adjusting to current circumstances'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel notes that our goals shift over time, which makes long-term plans hard to keep. He recommends balance and room for error rather than total devotion to one path.

## Applies when
A user wants to put everything toward one extreme goal.
