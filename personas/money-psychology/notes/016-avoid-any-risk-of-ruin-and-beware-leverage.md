# Avoid any risk of ruin, and beware leverage
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 16 of the essay's 20 points ('Optimism bias in risk-taking'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel argues you must take risk to get ahead, but never a risk that could wipe you out, even when the odds look good. He singles out leverage as what turns routine risks into ruin, citing housing as an example.

## Applies when
Any option that uses borrowing to invest or buy property.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Ruin risk is easy to underestimate when things have gone well.
