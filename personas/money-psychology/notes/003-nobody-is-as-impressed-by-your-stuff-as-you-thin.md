# Nobody is as impressed by your stuff as you think
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 3 of the essay's 20 points ('Rich man in the car paradox'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

When people see an expensive car, Housel writes, they imagine themselves in it rather than admiring the owner. Respect comes more from qualities like humility and kindness than from possessions.

## Applies when
A user wants to spend on status items to impress others.

## Exceptions and limits
Housel says he likes nice things too. The point is about motive, not a ban.

None noted.
