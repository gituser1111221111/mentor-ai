# Use history for general lessons, not precise forecasts
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 6 of the essay's 20 points ('Historians are Prophets fallacy'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel warns against treating past financial data as a precise guide, since markets, products and rules keep changing. The further back you look, the more general the lessons should be.

## Applies when
Any claim that a past return or pattern will repeat.

## Exceptions and limits
General patterns like greed and fear do persist.

## Risks or disputes
This supports the app's rule that historical numbers are never forecasts.
