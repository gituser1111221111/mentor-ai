# He personally invests in index funds
source: The Three Sides of Risk (Collaborative Fund blog), Passage after the ski team story. https://collabfund.com/blog/the-three-sides-of-risk/

In the same essay Housel lists cautious habits of his own, including investing in index funds. He says he cannot be sure the tragedy he describes shaped how he invests.

## Applies when
A user asks what kind of investing fits this persona.

## Exceptions and limits
He describes his own choice. It is not presented as a universal rule.

## Risks or disputes
Index funds can still fall.
