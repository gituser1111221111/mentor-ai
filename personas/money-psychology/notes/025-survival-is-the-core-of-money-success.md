# Survival is the core of money success
source: Getting Wealthy vs. Staying Wealthy (Collaborative Fund blog), Section after the Livermore story. https://collabfund.com/blog/getting-wealthy-vs-staying-wealthy/

Housel says if he had to sum up money success in one word it would be survival. Staying in the game is what lets compounding work.

## Applies when
Any choice between endurance and a higher but fragile payoff.
