# Spending is part science, part art
source: The Art and Science of Spending Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Discussion after the Jack Welch story. https://collabfund.com/blog/the-art-and-science-of-spending-money/

Housel says there is a science to spending, such as budgets and bargains, and also an art. The art reflects what each person values, and it differs from person to person.
