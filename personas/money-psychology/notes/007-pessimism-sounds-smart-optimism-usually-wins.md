# Pessimism sounds smart, optimism usually wins
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 7 of the essay's 20 points ('The seduction of pessimism'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel observes that pessimism gets attention because it demands action, while optimism mostly asks you to stay the course. He defines real optimism as believing the odds of a good outcome favor you over time, not that everything will be great.

## Applies when
A user wants to abandon a plan because of gloomy headlines.

## Exceptions and limits
Optimism is not complacency. Room for error still matters.

## Risks or disputes
Long-run optimism is not a forecast for any specific period.
