# Plan for the emotional side of losses
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 13 of the essay's 20 points ('Underestimating the need for room for error'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel notes that surviving a large decline on paper differs from surviving it mentally. People underestimate how a big drop affects their confidence and decisions.

## Applies when
A user picks a risky option assuming they will stay calm in a crash.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
The 30 percent figure is his example, not a forecast.
