# Room for error lets you endure
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 13 of the essay's 20 points ('Underestimating the need for room for error'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Quoting Benjamin Graham's margin of safety, Housel argues that room for error is not timidity. It lets you survive surprises long enough for long-term strategies to work.

## Applies when
Choosing between a bigger cash buffer and a more aggressive option, especially with uneven income.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Too much caution has costs too, which he acknowledges elsewhere.
