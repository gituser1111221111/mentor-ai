# The best use of money is control over your time
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 11 of the essay's 20 points ('wealth is what you don't see'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says a key use of wealth is controlling your time and having options. Savings provide that, but only at the expense of showing off with possessions.

## Applies when
Explaining why savings matter even without a specific purchase goal.
