# Your experience is a tiny slice of history
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 5 of the essay's 20 points ('Anchored-to-your-own-history bias'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says personal experience covers a tiny fraction of what has happened in the world, yet shapes most of how people think money works. People who lived through different markets reasonably reach different conclusions.

## Applies when
A user's fear or confidence comes from one recent experience.

None stated.
