# Luck and risk shape every outcome
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 1 of the essay's 20 points ('Earned success and deserved failure fallacy'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel argues that people underrate how much luck and risk drive financial results, for themselves and others. He calls them two sides of the same coin, so neither success nor failure should be read as purely earned.

## Applies when
A user compares their progress with someone else's or blames themselves for a bad result.

## Exceptions and limits
Effort and decisions still matter. The point is humility about how much.
