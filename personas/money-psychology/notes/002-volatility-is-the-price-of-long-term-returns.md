# Volatility is the price of long-term returns
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 2 of the essay's 20 points ('Cost avoidance syndrome'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says investment returns are not free. The price is volatility and uncertainty, and people who try to avoid that price through trading tricks or leverage often get caught.

## Applies when
A user wants investment growth without any chance of a drop.

## Exceptions and limits
Money needed soon should not be exposed to that price at all.

## Risks or disputes
Paying the price does not guarantee the reward.
