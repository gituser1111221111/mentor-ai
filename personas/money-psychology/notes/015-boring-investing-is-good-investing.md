# Boring investing is good investing
source: The Psychology of Money (Collaborative Fund blog), Point 15 of the essay's 20 points ('An attachment to financial entertainment'). https://collabfund.com/blog/the-psychology-of-money/

Housel says finance gets treated like entertainment because money is emotional. The goal of investing is returns, not avoiding boredom.

## Applies when
A user is drawn to exciting trades or constant market news.

None stated.
