# Invest 25 percent of gross income for retirement
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 8, Save 25% of Gross Income (23:45)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson states their savings rule. Invest 25 percent of gross income for retirement, counting employer contributions for many households.

## Applies when
A user asks how much to save for retirement.

## Exceptions and limits
People under 30 who start early may be able to save somewhat less. The rate can dip during big life changes.

## Risks or disputes
They acknowledge criticism that the number is high. It is a target, not a requirement.
