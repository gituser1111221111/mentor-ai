# Keep the emergency fund in high-yield savings
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'What account should my emergency fund be in?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide recommends a high-yield savings account for the emergency fund. It notes that online banks often pay more than branch banks.

## Applies when
Choosing where to hold the emergency fund.
