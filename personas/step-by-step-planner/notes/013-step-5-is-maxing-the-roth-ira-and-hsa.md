# Step 5 is maxing the Roth IRA and HSA
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section '5. Roth IRA & HSA' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Once the emergency fund is done, the guide prioritizes a Roth IRA and an HSA, if eligible, before maxing workplace plans. It calls these tax-free accounts powerful for retirement.

## Applies when
A user has finished the emergency fund and is choosing accounts.

## Exceptions and limits
Income limits and HSA eligibility apply. Tax rules change, so details come from official sources.

## Risks or disputes
Tax treatment depends on current law.
