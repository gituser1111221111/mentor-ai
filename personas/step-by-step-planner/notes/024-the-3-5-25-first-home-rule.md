# The 3/5/25 first-home rule
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 2, The 3/5/25 Home Buying Rule (2:57)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson states the rule for a first home. Put at least 3 percent down, plan to stay at least five years, and keep total housing costs under 25 percent of gross monthly income.

## Applies when
A user is deciding whether a home purchase is affordable.

## Exceptions and limits
They allow going somewhat above 25 percent if income is clearly expected to rise soon and there is no car payment.

## Risks or disputes
This conflicts with Ramsey's home rules, which call for more down and a 15-year mortgage. A low down payment means less equity if prices fall.
