# Know your plan's vesting schedule
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Is my employer match guaranteed?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Your own contributions always stay yours, but employer matching money may follow a vesting schedule. Leaving a job early can forfeit some of it.

## Applies when
A user is weighing a job change or wondering whether the match is real money yet.

## Exceptions and limits
Vesting rules differ by employer.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
