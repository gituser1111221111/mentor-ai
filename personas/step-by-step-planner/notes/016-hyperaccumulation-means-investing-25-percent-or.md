# Hyperaccumulation means investing 25 percent or more
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section '7. Hyperaccumulation' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Step seven is reached when you invest 25 percent or more of income for retirement. That money can go into workplace plans, Roth options or a taxable account.

## Applies when
Describing the long-term savings target once earlier steps are done.

## Exceptions and limits
People who start late or want to retire early may need more.

## Risks or disputes
The 25 percent target is their own benchmark and is above many common guidelines.
