# No financing luxury cars under 20/3/8
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 1, The 20/3/8 Car Buying Rule (0:34)', speaker line by Brian Preston. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Brian Preston adds that 20/3/8 is not a way to afford luxury cars. It exists so people can buy reliable, practical transportation without wrecking their wealth.

## Applies when
A user wants to finance an expensive or luxury vehicle.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.
