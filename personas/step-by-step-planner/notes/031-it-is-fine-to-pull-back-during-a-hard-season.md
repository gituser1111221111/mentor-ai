# It is fine to pull back during a hard season
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 8, Save 25% of Gross Income (23:45)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson says new kids, a move or a job change can force your savings rate down. That is okay as long as you shore yourself up and return to the target as soon as you can.

## Applies when
A user with a low-income month or major life change.
