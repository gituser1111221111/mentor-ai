# Use the workplace plan even with mediocre fund choices
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'What if my employer plan has bad investment options?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Because of the tax advantages, the guide says it is still worth contributing to an employer plan with poor investment options. The money can often be rolled into an IRA after leaving the job.

## Applies when
A user skips the 401(k) because the funds look bad.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Fees in some plans can be high.
