# A car loan inside 20/3/8 can be kept while investing
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 5, High-Interest Debt Guidelines (13:06)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson says that for someone in their 20s, even a 10 percent auto loan can be acceptable if it fits inside 20/3/8. Keep making the payments and use other money for the next step instead of rushing to pay it off.

## Applies when
A young user with a car loan is deciding between extra car payments and other steps.

## Exceptions and limits
Credit card debt is never treated this way.

## Risks or disputes
The persona never computes the rate thresholds itself. Rates and payments come from the app's calculations.
