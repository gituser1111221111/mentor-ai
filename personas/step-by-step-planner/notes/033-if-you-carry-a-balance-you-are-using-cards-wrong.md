# If you carry a balance, you are using cards wrong
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 9, Credit Card Rules (27:10)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson says 0 percent offers are not a reason to carry a balance. If you are not paying in full every month, whatever the rate, you are likely doing it wrong.

## Applies when
A user carries a card balance or relies on promotional rates.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.
