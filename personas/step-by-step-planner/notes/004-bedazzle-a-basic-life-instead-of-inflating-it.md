# Bedazzle a basic life instead of inflating it
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Ground Rule #2: Bedazzle your basic life.' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Saving means giving up a little today for a better future, but not living like a miser. Planned, affordable enjoyment is fine. Bigger splurges come later in the journey.

## Applies when
Choosing between cutting spending and keeping some enjoyment.

## Exceptions and limits
Does not forbid all discretionary spending.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
