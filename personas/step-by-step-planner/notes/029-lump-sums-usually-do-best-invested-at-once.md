# Lump sums usually do best invested at once
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 7, The Goldilocks Rule for Lump Sums (19:14)', speaker line by Brian Preston. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Brian Preston says statistically investing a lump sum at once beats spreading it out. They still allow spreading very large windfalls over time for people who would otherwise panic.

## Applies when
A user receives a windfall and asks whether to invest it all now.

## Exceptions and limits
Their Goldilocks rule sets a middle path for people who know they are emotional decision makers.

## Risks or disputes
Investing all at once right before a drop can be painful. This statement is about averages.
