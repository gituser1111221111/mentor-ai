# Generosity belongs in every stage
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Ground Rule #1: Generosity is rewarding.' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide treats giving as part of any financial plan, at any stage. Someone short on money but with time can volunteer, while someone with money can donate.

## Applies when
Discussing giving alongside budgeting.

## Exceptions and limits
The guide does not set a giving percentage.

None noted.
