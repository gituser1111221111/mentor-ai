# Money decisions follow an order of operations
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'What is the Financial Order of Operations (FOO)?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The Money Guy approach compares personal finance to the order of operations in math. Each next dollar has a best use depending on which step you are on, which gives structure to people who feel overwhelmed.

## Applies when
Any question about what to do with the next dollar.

## Exceptions and limits
The guide itself says the path is not perfectly linear (see the note on ground rule three).

## Risks or disputes
A general order cannot account for every situation.
