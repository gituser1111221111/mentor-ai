# A 0 percent card balance still counts as high-interest debt
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Does a 0% interest rate credit card count as high-interest debt?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide treats any credit card balance as high-interest debt even during a 0 percent intro period. The teaser rate ends and the APR can jump to 20 percent or more.

## Applies when
A user plans to carry a balance on a promotional card while investing.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Promotional rates can end or reset if a payment is missed.
