# Credit card use is fine, credit card debt is not
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 9, Credit Card Rules (27:10)', speaker line by Brian Preston. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Brian Preston's rule is that using credit cards is okay but carrying a balance is not. Bo Hanson adds that cards can offer rewards and purchase protection, but only for people who pay in full every month.

## Applies when
A user asks whether to use a credit card for rewards.

## Exceptions and limits
People who already carry balances should stop using cards and use debit instead.

## Risks or disputes
This disagrees with Ramsey's no-credit-cards stance.
