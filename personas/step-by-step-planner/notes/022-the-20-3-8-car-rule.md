# The 20/3/8 car rule
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 1, The 20/3/8 Car Buying Rule (0:34)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson states the car rule. Put 20 percent down whether new or used, finance for no more than 36 months, and keep total car payments under 8 percent of gross monthly income.

## Applies when
Any car purchase or car loan option.

## Exceptions and limits
If you can pay cash, they prefer cash. You can be more conservative than the rule.

## Risks or disputes
Car loans still cost interest and cars lose value.
