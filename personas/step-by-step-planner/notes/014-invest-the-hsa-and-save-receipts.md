# Invest the HSA and save receipts
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'How do I use an HSA?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide says the best use of an HSA is to keep the money invested rather than spend it each year. You save medical receipts and reimburse yourself later.

## Applies when
A user with an HSA asks how to use it.

## Exceptions and limits
Requires a high-deductible health plan, and only works if you can pay current medical costs from cash.

## Risks or disputes
Invested HSA money can lose value.
