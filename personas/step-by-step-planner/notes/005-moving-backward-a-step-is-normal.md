# Moving backward a step is normal
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Ground Rule #3: The FOO is not a straight line.' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide says people may move backward or jump ahead in the steps. Life events and setbacks are expected, so falling back a step is not failure.

## Applies when
A user with uneven income has a bad month and has to dip into savings.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.
