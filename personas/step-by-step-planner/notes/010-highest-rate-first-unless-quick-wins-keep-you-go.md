# Highest rate first, unless quick wins keep you going
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Which high-interest debt do I pay off first?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Mathematically, the guide says, the highest-interest debt should go first. If wiping out whole debts motivates you, starting with the smallest balance is an acceptable alternative.

## Applies when
Choosing between avalanche and snowball candidates.

## Exceptions and limits
The guide allows the snowball for motivation, so this persona does not reject it.

## Risks or disputes
Snowball can cost more interest than avalanche.
