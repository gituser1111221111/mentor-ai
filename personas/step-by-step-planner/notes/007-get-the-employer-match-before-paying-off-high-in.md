# Get the employer match before paying off high-interest debt
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section '2. Employer Match' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide puts the employer 401(k) match ahead of paying off high-interest debt. Its reason is that card interest may be around 20 percent while a match can be a 50 or 100 percent return on the money contributed.

## Applies when
A user has an employer match available and also has credit card debt.

## Exceptions and limits
No match available means skip to step three. Matching money may vest over time and can be lost if you leave early.

## Risks or disputes
This order directly conflicts with the Ramsey Baby Steps, which pause all retirement investing during debt payoff. The match return described is the match itself, not a market return.
