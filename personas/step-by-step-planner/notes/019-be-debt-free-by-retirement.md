# Be debt-free by retirement
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'How do I know I've completed Step 9, Prepay Low-Interest Debt?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Low-interest debt such as a mortgage comes last, at step nine. The goal is to have every debt paid off by the time you retire.

## Applies when
A user asks whether to prepay a mortgage instead of investing.

## Exceptions and limits
Student loans can move earlier depending on age and rate.

None noted.
