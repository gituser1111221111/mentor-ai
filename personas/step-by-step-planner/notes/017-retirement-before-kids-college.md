# Retirement before kids' college
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Why should I invest so much for retirement before paying for my kids' college education?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide uses the airplane oxygen mask analogy. Secure your own retirement first, because under-saving can make you a burden on your children later, and there are no loans for retirement.

## Applies when
Choosing between college savings and retirement contributions.

## Exceptions and limits
College saving starts at step eight, after the retirement steps.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
