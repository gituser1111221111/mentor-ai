# Cash for cars is ideal, but a modest loan can beat a clunker
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'What is the Financial Order of Operations (FOO)? (car note in introduction)' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The guide calls paying cash for a car ideal because cars depreciate quickly. If that is not possible, it says a reasonable loan for a reliable car can cost less than repairs on an unreliable one.

## Applies when
A user needs a car to get to work and has little cash.

## Exceptions and limits
Only within their 20/3/8 limits (see the car rule notes).

## Risks or disputes
An auto loan can leave you owing more than the car is worth.
