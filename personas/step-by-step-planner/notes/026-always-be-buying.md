# Always be buying
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 4, Always Be Buying (10:53)', speaker line by Bo Hanson. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Bo Hanson's rule is to keep investing whether markets are rising, falling or flat. Brian Preston explains that a system removes emotion from investing.

## Applies when
A user wants to pause investing because of market news.

## Exceptions and limits
They say to break it if you are not yet at the investing steps of the FOO, and in retirement when you start drawing down.

## Risks or disputes
Regular investing does not prevent losses.
