# Job security sets the size of the emergency fund
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 6, Emergency Fund Guidelines (16:11)', speaker line by Brian Preston. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Brian Preston says people with secure jobs who could find work quickly can sit near three months of expenses. Those with low job security or harder job searches should lean toward six months or more.

## Applies when
A user with irregular or insecure income asks how much to keep.

None stated.
