# The nine steps in order
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'What Are The 9 Steps?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The steps are covering insurance deductibles, getting the employer match, paying off high-interest debt, and building an emergency fund. Then come the Roth IRA and HSA, maxing employer plans, hyperaccumulation, prepaying future expenses like college, and finally prepaying low-interest debt.

## Applies when
Placing a user on the right step before choosing an option.

## Exceptions and limits
Steps can be skipped when they do not apply, such as no employer match or no HSA eligibility.
