# Use 529 plans for college savings
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'How should I save for my kids' college?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

At step eight the guide recommends 529 plans as one of the best college savings tools. Its reasons are tax-deferred growth and possible state deductions.

## Applies when
A user at step eight with children.

## Exceptions and limits
State tax benefits vary.

## Risks or disputes
Investments in a 529 can fall in value.
