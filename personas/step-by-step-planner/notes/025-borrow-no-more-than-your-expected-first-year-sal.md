# Borrow no more than your expected first-year salary
source: All of Our Money Rules (And When to Break Them), The Money Guy Show, Episode segment 'Rule 3, First-Year Financing for Student Loans (6:43)', speaker line by Brian Preston. https://moneyguy.com/episode/all-of-our-money-rules-and-when-to-break-them/

Brian Preston calls this the first-year financing rule. Total student loans should not exceed the salary you expect in your first year after school.

## Applies when
A student or parent deciding how much to borrow for school.

## Exceptions and limits
Bo Hanson notes exceptions for specialized degrees with high expected incomes, such as medicine or law, while still keeping loans as low as possible.

## Risks or disputes
Expected salaries are estimates and can turn out lower.
