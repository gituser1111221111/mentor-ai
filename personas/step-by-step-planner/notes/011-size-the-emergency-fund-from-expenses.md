# Size the emergency fund from expenses
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'How do I determine what to save in my emergency fund?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Step four builds an emergency fund of three to six months of expenses, sized from what you spend, not what you earn. A single income or a specialized field where jobs are scarce calls for more.

## Applies when
Deciding how big a full emergency fund should be.

## Exceptions and limits
Step four comes after the match and high-interest debt in this order.

## Risks or disputes
Too little cash leaves you exposed to a job loss. Too much has an opportunity cost.
