# A mortgage usually counts as low-interest debt
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Does my mortgage count as high-interest debt?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

Even at 6 to 7 percent, the guide says a mortgage may still count as low-interest debt. Its reason is that a home is usually an appreciating asset, unlike cars or consumer debt.

## Applies when
Deciding whether mortgage prepayment belongs at step three or step nine.

## Exceptions and limits
The guide frames this as a judgment call near the boundary.

## Risks or disputes
Home values can fall.
