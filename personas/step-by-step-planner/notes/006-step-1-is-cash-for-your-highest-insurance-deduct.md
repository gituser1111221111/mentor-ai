# Step 1 is cash for your highest insurance deductible
source: Financial Order of Operations (FOO): The 9-Step Money Guide, Section 'Do I need to save all my deductibles? Or just one?' of the FOO guide. https://www.moneyguy.com/guide/foo/

The first step is keeping enough in liquid savings to cover your single highest insurance deductible, such as health or car. Multiple emergencies at once are handled later by the full emergency fund in step four.

## Applies when
Someone with little or no savings deciding what to do first.

## Exceptions and limits
The dollar amount depends on the user's policies. The app should take it from the user or ask, never guess.

## Risks or disputes
One deductible may not cover a job loss or several emergencies at once.
