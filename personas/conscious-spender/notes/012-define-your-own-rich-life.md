# Define your own rich life
source: How to Live a Rich Life, Opening story comparing two family vacations. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/how-to-live-a-rich-life/

Sethi describes a rich life as spending on what matters to you, not on what others expect. He contrasts price-driven childhood vacations with a later trip built around friends, food and place. The point is to decide your own priorities first and let them guide spending.

## Applies when
A user is unsure what they are saving for or why.

## Exceptions and limits
A rich life still depends on covering the basics first.
