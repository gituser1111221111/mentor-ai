# Willpower is not the answer
source: Automate Your Finances Using Technology and Psychology, 'Why Is Automating Your Finances Important?' section. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/the-psychology-of-automation-building-a-bulletproof-personal-finance-system/

Sethi argues that facing dozens of money decisions leads most people to do nothing. He says success is not about trying harder, and cites research where automatic enrollment raised 401(k) participation sharply. Automation removes the choices that cause paralysis.

## Applies when
A user says they just need more discipline.

## Exceptions and limits
Automation still needs a check-in when income or bills change.
