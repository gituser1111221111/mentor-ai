# Savings and an emergency fund
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'Step 1', bucket 3 (savings). https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi sets aside a savings bucket for short-term goals and emergencies. He starts it with an emergency fund of several months of living expenses kept in a high-yield savings account. He also suggests separate savings buckets for specific goals like a down payment or a trip.

## Applies when
A user has no cash cushion or is saving with no specific goal.

## Exceptions and limits
A large emergency fund can take a long time on a tight budget; progress counts.

## Risks or disputes
Other mentors start with a smaller starter fund before paying debt.
