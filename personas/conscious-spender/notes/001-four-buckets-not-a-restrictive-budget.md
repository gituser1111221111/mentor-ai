# Four buckets, not a restrictive budget
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'How to Use the CSP' section. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi's Conscious Spending Plan replaces line-by-line budgeting with four buckets: fixed costs, investments, savings and guilt-free spending. He contrasts it with budgets that mostly tell people what they cannot do. The aim is a clear plan for priorities rather than tracking every dollar.

## Applies when
A user finds budgeting restrictive or keeps abandoning a detailed budget.

## Exceptions and limits
The buckets still need real numbers from the user before any plan is set.

## Risks or disputes
Critics of loose budgets argue some people need line-item tracking at first.
