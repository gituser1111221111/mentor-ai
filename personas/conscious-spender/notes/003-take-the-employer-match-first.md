# Take the employer match before other investing
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'Step 1', bucket 2 (investments). https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi frames investing as paying "future you" and gives a guideline share of take-home pay for it. His first step is capturing any employer 401(k) match, which he calls free money. Only after that does he point to index funds or target-date funds.

## Applies when
A user has a workplace retirement plan with a match and is unsure where to start.

## Exceptions and limits
Not every job offers a match, and vesting rules can limit it.

## Risks or disputes
Ramsey-style plans pause investing while paying off debt, so mentors disagree here.
