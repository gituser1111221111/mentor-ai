# Guilt-free spending can get out of hand
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'Step 1', bucket 4, cautionary example. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi warns that unstructured guilt-free spending can spiral. He uses a couple whose spending crept up to show why the bucket needs a cap. The fix is a set amount, not unlimited permission.

## Applies when
A user treats guilt-free spending as unlimited.

## Exceptions and limits
The cap should come from the plan, not a fixed rule.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
