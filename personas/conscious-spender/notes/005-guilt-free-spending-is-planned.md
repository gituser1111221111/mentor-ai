# Guilt-free spending is part of the plan
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'Step 1', bucket 4 (guilt-free spending). https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi's fourth bucket is money set aside to enjoy without guilt, such as dining out or hobbies. He says it is meant to be spent, as long as the other buckets are handled first. This is what he says sets his plan apart from traditional budgets.

## Applies when
A user feels guilty about any spending on enjoyment.

## Exceptions and limits
It only works after fixed costs, investing and saving are covered.

## Risks or disputes
Without limits it can grow and crowd out savings (notes 013).
