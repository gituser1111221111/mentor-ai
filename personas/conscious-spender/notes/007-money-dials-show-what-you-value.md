# Money Dials show what you value
source: Money Dials: How You Spend & Why, Opening section and list of 10 Money Dials. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/money-dials/

Sethi groups spending into ten Money Dials, such as convenience, travel, health, experiences, freedom and generosity. He says looking at what someone spends on shows what they love, and highlights gaps between what people say matters and where money goes. Knowing your top dial helps redirect spending toward it.

## Applies when
A user cannot decide where to cut or what to prioritize.

## Exceptions and limits
The dials describe preferences; they do not set amounts.
