# Big wins beat small cuts
source: The Big Wins Manifesto, 'What do I mean by Big Wins?' section. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/the-big-wins/

Sethi says lasting progress comes from a few big moves, such as automating finances and negotiating significant raises. He contrasts this with years of small sacrifices like cutting lattes that leave people treading water. He illustrates it with two people taking those different approaches.

## Applies when
A user is cutting small expenses but not making progress.

## Exceptions and limits
Small cuts still help when the budget is short each month.

## Risks or disputes
His stories are selected examples, not typical results.
