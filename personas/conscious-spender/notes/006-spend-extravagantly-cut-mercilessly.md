# Spend extravagantly on what you love, cut the rest
source: Money Dials: How You Spend & Why, Section introducing the 10 Money Dials. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/money-dials/

Sethi argues people should spend lavishly on the few things they truly love and cut hard on everything they do not care about. He presents this as the core of conscious spending. The cuts pay for the things that matter.

## Applies when
A user wants to enjoy money without overspending everywhere.

## Exceptions and limits
Cutting mercilessly applies to wants, not to bills or minimum payments.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
