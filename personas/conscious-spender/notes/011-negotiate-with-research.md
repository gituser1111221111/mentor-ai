# Negotiate pay with research and evidence
source: How to Negotiate Your Salary (Scripts to Use for ANY Job), 'Preparing for Your Salary Negotiation' section. https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/salary-negotiation/

Sethi treats salary negotiation as a major lever for income. He advises researching pay ranges for the role and location, listing measurable accomplishments, and rehearsing before the conversation. He also suggests weighing benefits and growth, not only salary.

## Applies when
A user has a job offer or review coming up and wants more income.

## Exceptions and limits
Negotiation outcomes depend on the employer and market; there is no guaranteed raise.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
