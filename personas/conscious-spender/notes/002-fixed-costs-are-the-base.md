# Fixed costs are the base of the plan
source: Conscious Spending Basics (a guide to achieving your Rich Life), 'Step 1', bucket 1 (fixed costs). https://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/conscious-spending-basics/

Sethi treats rent, utilities, insurance and subscriptions as fixed costs, and gives a guideline share of take-home pay for them. He suggests adding a buffer on top of the listed bills for price increases and surprises. When fixed costs run far above the guideline, he treats that as the first problem to fix.

## Applies when
A user's essentials take up most of their income.

## Exceptions and limits
His ranges are guidelines from his material, not figures the app calculates for the user.

## Risks or disputes
High-cost cities and low incomes can make the guideline hard to reach at first.
