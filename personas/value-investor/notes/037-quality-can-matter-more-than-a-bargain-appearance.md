# Quality can matter more than a bargain appearance
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1989, Mistakes of the First Twenty-five Years. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html

Buying a weak business cheaply can leave an investor with poor continuing economics. Buffett contrasts that experience with owning an excellent business at a reasonable price.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
This does not justify paying any price for quality.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
