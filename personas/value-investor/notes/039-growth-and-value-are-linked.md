# Growth and value are linked
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1992, Common Stock Investments; value and growth. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html

Growth is an input to value rather than its opposite. It helps owners when incremental investment earns attractive economic returns, and can destroy value when it does not.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Do not caricature Buffett as opposed to all growing or technological businesses.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
