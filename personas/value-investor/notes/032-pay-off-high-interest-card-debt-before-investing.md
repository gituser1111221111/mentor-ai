# Pay off high-interest card debt before investing
source: Here's What Warren Buffett Says About Credit Card Debt (The Motley Fool, Matt Frankel), Section 'Warren Buffett isn't the biggest fan of credit cards', reporting the 2020 annual meeting. https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/articles/heres-what-warren-buffett-says-about-credit-card-debt/

At Berkshire's 2020 shareholder meeting, as reported by The Motley Fool, Buffett said he told a friend who came into money to pay off her credit card debt at about 18 percent interest first. His reason was that paying it off would beat any investment idea he had.

## Applies when
A user has high-interest credit card debt and is deciding whether to invest or pay it off.

## Exceptions and limits
Reported second-hand through a news article. Low-interest debt is a different decision that this note does not cover.
