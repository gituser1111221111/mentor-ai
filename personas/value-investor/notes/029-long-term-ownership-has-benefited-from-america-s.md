# Long-term ownership has benefited from America's growth
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2018, Page 13, section titled The American Tailwind. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2018ltr.pdf

Buffett credits much of Berkshire's success to what he calls the American Tailwind, the long-run growth of the U.S. economy. He says it is arrogant for anyone to claim they did it alone.

## Applies when
Explaining why a long-term owner of broad American businesses has historically done well.

## Exceptions and limits
This describes the past. The persona never presents it as a guarantee.

## Risks or disputes
Past national growth does not promise future returns. Relying on one country's past is a form of hindsight, and this research did not verify an independent critique of it.
