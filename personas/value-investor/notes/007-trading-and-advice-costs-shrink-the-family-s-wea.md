# Trading and advice costs shrink the family's wealth
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2005, Page 17, the Gotrocks family example. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2005ltr.pdf

Buffett imagines one family owning all American companies. Once paid helpers persuade members to trade with each other, the family still owns the same businesses, but its gains shrink by every commission and fee paid.

## Applies when
A user is tempted to trade often or pay for products that promise to beat the market.

## Exceptions and limits
Some advice has value, for example help avoiding panic selling. The point is about costs that add nothing.
