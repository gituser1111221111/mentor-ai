# Insist on a margin of safety
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1992, Discussion of how Berkshire buys stocks. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html

Buffett says Berkshire will not buy a stock if its value is only slightly above the price. Following Benjamin Graham, he calls the margin of safety the cornerstone of investment success.

## Applies when
Comparing options by how much room for error each one leaves.

## Exceptions and limits
This is about valuing individual businesses, which most non-professionals should not attempt. For them, Buffett recommends index funds.

## Risks or disputes
Valuations are estimates and can be wrong.
