# Never bet against America
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2021, Page 7, story of his first stock purchase. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2021ltr.pdf

Recalling his first stock purchase in 1942, made with all his savings while the Dow stood at 99, Buffett draws a lesson about long-term optimism. He urges readers never to bet against America.

## Applies when
A long-term saver is scared out of investing by bad news.

## Exceptions and limits
This is an expression of long-term optimism, not a forecast for any period.

## Risks or disputes
Optimism about the past is not a return projection.
