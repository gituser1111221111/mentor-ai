# Leverage turned a farm price boom into ruin
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2013, Page 16, the Nebraska farm example. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf

Buffett recalls the Midwest farm boom of the 1970s, fueled by expectations of inflation and easy bank lending. When prices fell by half or more, leveraged farmers and their lenders were devastated.

## Applies when
A real estate or asset purchase relies on heavy borrowing and rising prices.

## Exceptions and limits
Buffett himself bought a farm in that period without describing it as a mistake. The lesson is about leverage and bubble pricing.

## Risks or disputes
Borrowing against an asset whose price can fall can wipe out the owner.
