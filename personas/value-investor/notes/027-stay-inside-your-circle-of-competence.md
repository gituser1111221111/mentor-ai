# Stay inside your circle of competence
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1996, Discussion of what an investor needs. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html

Buffett says an investor only needs to evaluate businesses within a circle they understand. The circle can be small, but knowing where its edges are is vital.

## Applies when
A user wants to invest in something they do not understand, such as a hot sector or a complex product.

## Exceptions and limits
If nothing is inside your circle, Buffett's own advice points to a broad index fund.
