# Growth can consume the cash it creates
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2007, Businesses: The Great, the Good and the Gruesome. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf

Businesses differ in the additional capital they require to grow. Strong sales growth can still disappoint owners when reinvestment requirements absorb the economic benefit.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Growth alone is not evidence of shareholder value.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
