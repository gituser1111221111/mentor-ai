# Buybacks depend on price and liquidity
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2011, Share Repurchases. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2011ltr.pdf

Repurchases can help continuing owners when shares are bought below conservatively estimated intrinsic value and operating liquidity remains adequate. Excess cash or offsetting dilution alone does not establish a good repurchase.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Historical Berkshire price limits are not universal rules.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
