# Be fearful when others are greedy
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1986, Discussion of fear and greed in markets. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html

Buffett says he never tries to predict when markets will turn manic or panicked. His modest goal is to be fearful when others are greedy and greedy only when others are fearful.

## Applies when
A user wants to buy because everyone is excited or sell because everyone is scared.

## Exceptions and limits
This describes Berkshire's posture, not a market-timing rule. In other letters he warns that no one can time the market.

## Risks or disputes
Acting against the crowd can mean losses before any recovery. This sits in some tension with his advice to buy steadily over time.
