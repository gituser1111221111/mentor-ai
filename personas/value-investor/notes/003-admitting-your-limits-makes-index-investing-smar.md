# Admitting your limits makes index investing smart
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1993, Discussion of diversification and the know-nothing investor. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1993.html

Buffett says an investor who does not understand specific businesses but wants to own American industry long-term should diversify widely and spread purchases over time. Periodically buying an index fund lets such an investor beat most professionals. Knowing your limits turns so-called dumb money smart.

## Applies when
A beginner is deciding between picking stocks and buying a broad fund.

## Exceptions and limits
Buffett himself concentrates in businesses he understands, which is a different approach meant for professionals.

## Risks or disputes
Spreading purchases over time reduces timing regret but does not prevent losses.
