# Recognize the boundary of what you understand
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1996, Common Stock Investments; circle of competence. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html

Investors need not evaluate every business. Knowing the boundary of their competence matters more than the size of the opportunity set.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Passing is a valid research conclusion.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
