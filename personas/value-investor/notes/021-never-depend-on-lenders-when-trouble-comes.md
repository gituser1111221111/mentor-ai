# Never depend on lenders when trouble comes
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 6, discussion of financial strength. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett says he will never run Berkshire in a way that depends on the kindness of strangers or of friends who may have their own cash problems. During the 2008 crisis, holding large amounts of Treasury bills meant Berkshire never had to rely on bank credit.

## Applies when
Explaining why a cash reserve matters more than access to credit.

## Exceptions and limits
This is said about running a company. Applying it to a household budget is an interpretation by the persona.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
