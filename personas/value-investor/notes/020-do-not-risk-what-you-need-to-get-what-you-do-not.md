# Do not risk what you need to get what you do not need
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 3, explanation of Berkshire's low use of debt. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett writes that avoiding leverage has lowered Berkshire's returns, but he and Charlie Munger sleep well. Both believe it is insane to risk what you have and need to get what you do not need.

## Applies when
Any option that risks essentials, savings or housing to chase a higher return.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Avoiding debt can mean lower returns than a leveraged strategy in good years. He accepts that trade.
