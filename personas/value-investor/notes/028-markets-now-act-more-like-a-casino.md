# Markets now act more like a casino
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2023, Page 6, discussion of today's market. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf

Buffett writes that today's market participants are no more emotionally stable than in his youth. He says markets now show far more casino-like behavior, and the casino now sits in many homes, tempting people daily.

## Applies when
A user is drawn to frequent trading, speculation or apps that make betting easy.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Speculative trading can lose money quickly.
