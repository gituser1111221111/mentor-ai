# Money you need soon belongs in cash, not stocks
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 17, section on volatility and risk. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett notes that anyone who might have meaningful near-term needs for funds should keep appropriate amounts in Treasuries or insured bank deposits. Only money with a multi-decade horizon can ignore price drops.

## Applies when
Choosing between an emergency fund and investing, or deciding where to keep money for upcoming bills.

## Exceptions and limits
He does not give a specific number of months. The amount comes from the app's calculations, not from this note.

## Risks or disputes
Cash loses value to inflation over long periods, so keeping too much in cash has its own cost.
