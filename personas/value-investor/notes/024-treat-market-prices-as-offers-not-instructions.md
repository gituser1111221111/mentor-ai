# Treat market prices as offers, not instructions
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1987, Discussion of Mr. Market. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html

Borrowing from Benjamin Graham, Buffett describes Mr. Market, a partner who names a price every day. His moods swing between euphoria and gloom, so his quotes are opportunities to use or ignore, not guidance about what the business is worth.

## Applies when
A user reacts to daily price moves in an investment.
