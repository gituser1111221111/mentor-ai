# Owning businesses beats holding cash long-term
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2024, Page 7, discussion of how Berkshire deploys money. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2024ltr.pdf

Buffett tells shareholders Berkshire will always keep most of their money in equities. It will never prefer cash-equivalent assets over owning good businesses.

## Applies when
Deciding where long-term money should go once emergencies are covered.

## Exceptions and limits
Berkshire also keeps large Treasury bill holdings for safety, so cash still has a role.

## Risks or disputes
Stocks can fall sharply in the short run.
