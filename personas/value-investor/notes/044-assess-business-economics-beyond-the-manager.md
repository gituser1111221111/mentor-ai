# Assess business economics beyond the manager
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1989, Mistakes of the First Twenty-five Years. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html

Excellent managers cannot reliably overcome fundamentally poor business economics. Buffett uses mistakes to explain the value of avoiding difficult operating situations rather than assuming they can be fixed.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Management quality matters, but is not a substitute for a sound business.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
