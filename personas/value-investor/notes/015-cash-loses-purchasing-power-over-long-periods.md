# Cash loses purchasing power over long periods
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 17, section on volatility and risk. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett points out that from 1965 to 2014 the dollar lost most of its purchasing power. He defines investing as giving up purchasing power now to reasonably expect more purchasing power later, after taxes.

## Applies when
Deciding whether long-term money should sit in cash.

## Exceptions and limits
Cash remains right for emergencies and near-term needs.

## Risks or disputes
The 87 percent figure is a historical measurement for one period, not a forecast of future inflation.
