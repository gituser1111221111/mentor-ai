# Stocks can fall far and fast, so do not borrow to own them
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 9, table of Berkshire's major price declines. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett shows four periods when Berkshire shares fell between about 37 and 59 percent. He calls this his strongest argument against ever borrowing to own stocks. Even small borrowings can rattle your thinking during a crash.

## Applies when
Any option involving margin, borrowing against assets to invest, or leveraged products.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
These are historical declines in one stock. They show what is possible, not what will happen.
