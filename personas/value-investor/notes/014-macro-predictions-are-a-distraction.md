# Macro predictions are a distraction
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2013, Page 17, section on investing for non-professionals. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf

Buffett writes that forming macro opinions or listening to other people's market predictions wastes time. He adds that it can be dangerous because it blurs your view of the facts that actually matter.

## Applies when
A decision is being driven by headlines or predictions rather than the person's own numbers and timeline.

None stated.
