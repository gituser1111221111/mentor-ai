# Fear of price swings can lead to riskier choices
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 18, section on volatility and risk. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett says investors who fear volatility can end up doing risky things. He points to people who moved to supposedly safe Treasury bills or CDs after the 2008 drop and later earned very little on money meant to fund retirement.

## Applies when
A long-term saver wants to stop investing after a market drop.

## Exceptions and limits
Short-term money is different. See the note on near-term needs.

## Risks or disputes
Staying invested through a drop can be painful, and markets can stay down for years.
