# Ignore market forecasts
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 18, the list of investor mistakes. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett says no adviser, economist, TV commentator, or he himself can tell you when market chaos will happen. Forecasts may be entertaining but will not make you money.

## Applies when
A user asks whether to wait for a market drop or act on a prediction.
