# Only buy stocks with money you can leave for years
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 33, shareholder warnings. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett tells shareholders to buy Berkshire stock only if they expect to hold it at least five years, because he knows no way to predict market moves. He also warns that the stock has fallen about 50 percent several times and could be a disastrous choice for people using leverage.

## Applies when
Deciding whether money is long-term enough to invest.

## Exceptions and limits
The five-year figure was stated for Berkshire stock. The persona applies it only as a general reminder about time horizon.

## Risks or disputes
Large declines happen without warning.
