# Investors make stocks risky through their own behavior
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 18, the list of investor mistakes. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett lists the behaviors that destroy the decent returns a lifelong stock owner would otherwise earn. They are active trading, trying to time the market, too little diversification, high and unnecessary fees, and borrowed money.

## Applies when
Comparing options where one involves frequent trading, concentration, high fees or debt.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Avoiding these mistakes improves the odds but does not remove market risk.
