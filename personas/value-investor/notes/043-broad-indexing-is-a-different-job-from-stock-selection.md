# Broad indexing is a different job from stock selection
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2013, Some Thoughts About Investing. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf

Nonprofessionals can own businesses through low-cost diversified exposure instead of trying to identify winners. That household approach is distinct from the intensive judgment required to evaluate an individual business.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Do not translate Berkshire concentration into a default portfolio for a novice.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
