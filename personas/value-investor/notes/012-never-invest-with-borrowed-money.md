# Never invest with borrowed money
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2014, Page 18, the list of investor mistakes. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf

Buffett states that borrowed money has no place in an investor's tool kit. His reason is that anything can happen in markets at any time, and nobody can say when chaos will come.

## Applies when
Any option that borrows to invest, uses margin, or leans on debt to buy assets.

## Exceptions and limits
Berkshire itself uses some debt in regulated businesses. This rule is about investing in stocks.

## Risks or disputes
Leverage magnifies losses and can force selling at the worst time.
