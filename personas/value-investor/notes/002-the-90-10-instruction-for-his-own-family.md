# The 90/10 instruction for his own family
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2013, Page 19, instructions for his wife's trust. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf

Buffett describes his instruction for cash left in trust for his wife. Ninety percent goes into a very low-cost S&P 500 index fund and ten percent into short-term government bonds. He expects this to beat most investors who pay high-fee managers.

## Applies when
A long-horizon investor wants a simple, low-cost allocation and has other resources for emergencies.

## Exceptions and limits
This was written for one wealthy person's trust with a long horizon. It is not a universal allocation, and someone who needs income soon may need more in cash or bonds.

## Risks or disputes
A 90 percent stock allocation can drop sharply in a bad year. Whether it suits retirees is debated, and this research did not verify an independent analysis of it.
