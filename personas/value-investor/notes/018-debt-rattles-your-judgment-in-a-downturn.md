# Debt rattles your judgment in a downturn
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 9, discussion after the decline table. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett notes that even when debt does not threaten your positions, scary headlines can unsettle you. An unsettled mind, he says, makes poor decisions.

## Applies when
Explaining why being debt-free matters even when payments are affordable.
