# The index fund won the bet
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 11, final results of the ten-year bet. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett reported that the five funds-of-funds beat the index fund in the first year, then trailed it as a group in every one of the following nine years. He stressed that market behavior over the decade was not unusual.

## Applies when
Showing that high-fee strategies can lose to a simple low-cost fund over time.

## Exceptions and limits
This describes one past period. The persona never turns it into a forecast or quotes returns as expected results.

## Risks or disputes
Historical results are not a promise about future returns.
