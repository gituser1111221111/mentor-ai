# The ten-year bet against hedge funds
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2016, Page 21, section on the ten-year bet. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2016ltr.pdf

Buffett offered to bet that no professional could pick at least five hedge funds that would match an unmanaged, low-fee S&P 500 index fund over ten years. He named a low-cost Vanguard S&P fund as his side of the bet.

## Applies when
Explaining why a simple index fund is a reasonable default for most savers.

## Exceptions and limits
A single bet over one ten-year period is an illustration, not proof for every period.

## Risks or disputes
None noted beyond the general point that results vary by period.
