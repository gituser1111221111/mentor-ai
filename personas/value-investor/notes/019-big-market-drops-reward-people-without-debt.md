# Big market drops reward people without debt
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 2017, Page 10, discussion after the decline table. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2017ltr.pdf

Buffett writes that major declines will happen again with no warning. When they do, they are extraordinary opportunities for investors who are not handicapped by debt.

## Applies when
Comparing paying off debt with investing, for someone who wants to be able to act calmly in a downturn.

## Exceptions and limits
This is not advice to time the market. Buffett says no one can tell when declines happen.

## Risks or disputes
Buying during declines can still lose money if prices keep falling.
