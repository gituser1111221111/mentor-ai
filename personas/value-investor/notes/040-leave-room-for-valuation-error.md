# Leave room for valuation error
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1992, Common Stock Investments; margin of safety. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html

An estimate only slightly above price is not enough for this approach. Uncertainty in the business and its future cash generation makes a margin of safety important.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
The notes do not provide a universal discount threshold.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
