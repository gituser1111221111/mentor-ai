# Owner earnings require maintenance reinvestment
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1986, Appendix: Purchase-Price Accounting Adjustments. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html

Owner earnings adjust accounting profits for noncash charges and the spending needed to sustain the business. Maintenance capital and working capital requirements are estimates, so accounting precision can conceal economic uncertainty.

## Applies when
Qualitative company research and discussion of the mentor perspective.

## Exceptions and limits
Do not equate EBITDA with distributable cash.

## Risks or disputes
Application guardrail: test the thesis against contrary evidence; this research principle does not establish suitability or promise returns.
