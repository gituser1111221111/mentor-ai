# Hold great businesses for the long run
source: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter for 1988, Discussion of permanent holdings. https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1988.html

Buffett says that when Berkshire owns parts of outstanding businesses with outstanding management, its favorite holding period is forever. He contrasts this with people who sell winners quickly and hold onto losers.

## Applies when
Discouraging frequent trading of long-term holdings.

## Exceptions and limits
Stated for outstanding businesses with good management, not for every holding.

## Risks or disputes
Holding forever does not protect against a business declining.
