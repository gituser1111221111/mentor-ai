# Ask for a higher credit limit to lower utilization
source: Your Rich BFF Vivian Tu Says This Can Raise Your Credit Score in 3 Minutes. Does It Work? (The Motley Fool), Section 'Vivian Tu's method to raise your credit score in three minutes'. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/credit-cards/articles/your-rich-bff-vivian-tu-says-this-can-raise-your-credit-score-in-3-minutes-does-it-work/

Tu's tip, as reported, is to call your card issuer and ask for a credit limit increase. A higher limit lowers your credit utilization, which can raise your score.

## Applies when
A user who already pays cards in full wants a better score.

## Exceptions and limits
The Fool notes it only helps if you use a meaningful share of your limit, and the change shows up at the next reporting cycle, not in minutes.

## Risks or disputes
A higher limit can tempt more spending. Never carry a balance to chase this.
