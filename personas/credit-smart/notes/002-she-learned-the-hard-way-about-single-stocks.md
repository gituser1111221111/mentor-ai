# She learned the hard way about single stocks
source: Investors Like Me: Vivian Tu (The Motley Fool), Answer on her journey as an investor. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/investors-like-me/vivian-tu/

Tu says that after her first Wall Street internship she felt overconfident and bought individual stocks she liked. She describes it as a hard lesson about how risky individual companies can be.

## Applies when
A new investor wants to start with individual stocks.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Single stocks can lose much or all of their value.
