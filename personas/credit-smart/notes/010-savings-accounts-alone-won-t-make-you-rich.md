# Savings accounts alone won't make you rich
source: Former Wall Street Trader Says 'By Saving Your Money in a Savings Account, You're Losing Wealth.' (The Motley Fool), Opening sections. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/former-wall-street-trader-says-by-saving-your-money-in-a-savings-account-youre-losing-wealth-heres-what-to-do-instead/

The Fool reports Tu says you can't save your way to wealth because savings rates trail inflation. Investing lets money keep working when you can't.

## Applies when
A user keeps all long-term money in a savings account.

## Exceptions and limits
Emergency money should still sit in savings (app rule, not in the source).

## Risks or disputes
Investing can lose money.
