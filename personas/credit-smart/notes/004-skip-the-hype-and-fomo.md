# Skip the hype and FOMO
source: Investors Like Me: Vivian Tu (The Motley Fool), Answer on what scares her about the future of investing. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/investors-like-me/vivian-tu/

Tu says hype and fear of missing out lead people to chase the newest trend without understanding it, such as obscure cryptocurrencies or options. She says that has never worked out.

## Applies when
A user wants to jump into a trending asset.

## Exceptions and limits
None stated.

## Risks or disputes
Speculative assets can lose most of their value quickly.
