# Step one is picking the right account
source: Your Rich BFF's Vivian Tu Breaks Down 2 Easy Steps to Invest (The Motley Fool), Section on investment accounts. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/buying-stocks/articles/your-rich-bffs-vivian-tu-breaks-down-2-easy-steps-to-invest/

The Fool reports Tu's first step for new investors is opening an account, either a tax-advantaged retirement account or a regular brokerage account. She calls them online tote bags that hold your investments.

## Applies when
A beginner doesn't know where to start investing.

## Exceptions and limits
Retirement accounts have withdrawal rules and penalties. Details come from official sources.
