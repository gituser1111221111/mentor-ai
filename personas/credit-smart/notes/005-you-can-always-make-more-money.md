# You can always make more money
source: Investors Like Me: Vivian Tu (The Motley Fool), Answer on a quote that inspires her. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/investors-like-me/vivian-tu/

Tu calls a mentor's advice the best money advice she ever got. You can only save as much as you earn, but you can always earn more.

## Applies when
Choosing between cutting spending and raising income.

## Exceptions and limits
The quote originates with her mentor. She endorses it.

## Risks or disputes
None noted.
