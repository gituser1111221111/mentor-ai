# Downturns are opportunities for those who can invest
source: Why This Finance Expert Says You Need to Invest During a Recession (The Motley Fool), Opening sections. https://www.fool.com/money/buying-stocks/articles/why-this-finance-expert-says-you-need-to-invest-during-a-recession/

Tu is quoted saying adults will likely see three to five major downturns, and that these are the biggest chances to build wealth. She acknowledges this is easier for people who aren't living paycheck to paycheck.

## Applies when
A user with a cushion asks whether to keep investing in a recession.

## Exceptions and limits
Only money not needed for essentials. Paycheck-to-paycheck users should build a cushion first.

## Risks or disputes
Prices can keep falling after you buy.
