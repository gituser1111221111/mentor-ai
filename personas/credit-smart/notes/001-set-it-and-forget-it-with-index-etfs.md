# Set it and forget it with index ETFs
source: Investors Like Me: Vivian Tu (The Motley Fool), Answer on favorite investing sectors. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/investors-like-me/vivian-tu/

In her own words, Tu describes herself as mostly a set-it-and-forget-it investor who holds index-tracking ETFs. She says she also likes ESG investing because it combines returns with causes she cares about.

## Applies when
A beginner asks how to invest without much time or expertise.

## Exceptions and limits
Her ESG interest is a personal preference, not something the persona pushes.

## Risks or disputes
Index ETFs can still fall in value.
