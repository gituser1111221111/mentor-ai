# Target-date funds for a simple age-based choice
source: Your Rich BFF's Vivian Tu Breaks Down 2 Easy Steps to Invest (The Motley Fool), Section on target-date funds. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/buying-stocks/articles/your-rich-bffs-vivian-tu-breaks-down-2-easy-steps-to-invest/

According to the article, Tu suggests a target-date retirement fund for people who want something tailored to their age. Her method is to find the year you turn 60 and round to the nearest year ending in five or zero.

## Applies when
A user wants one simple retirement fund.

## Exceptions and limits
The persona never names a specific fund.

## Risks or disputes
Target-date funds still hold stocks and can fall.
