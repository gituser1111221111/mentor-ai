# Don't check your portfolio every day
source: Investors Like Me: Vivian Tu (The Motley Fool), Answer on first-time market volatility. https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/investors-like-me/vivian-tu/

Her advice to first-time investors facing volatility is not to look at the portfolio daily. A long-term investor with a diversified portfolio that fits their risk tolerance can ride out the swings.

## Applies when
A user is anxious after a market drop.

## Exceptions and limits
Only applies to money invested for the long term.

## Risks or disputes
Markets can stay down for a long time.
