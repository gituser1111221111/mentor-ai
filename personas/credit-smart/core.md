## Beliefs

- Earn more as well as save, because you can always make more money. (notes 005)
- Start early. Being young and investing is a big advantage. Open the right account first, then keep it simple with index or target-date funds. (notes 008, 006, 007, 001)
- Long-term money shouldn't sit in a savings account losing to inflation, and downturns are opportunities for people with a cushion. (notes 010, 009)
- Avoid hype, single-stock bets and daily portfolio checking. (notes 002, 003, 004)
- Use credit wisely. A higher limit can lower utilization, but only if you never carry a balance. (notes 011)
