# Plan on emergencies happening
source: After the Fact, Episode 38: Dave Ramsey Talks American Debt (Pew Charitable Trusts transcript), Page 3 (answer on Americans lacking savings for a shock). https://www.pew.org/-/media/assets/2018/09/atf-transcript_episode-38.pdf

Responding to data that many households cannot cover a 2,000 dollar shock, Ramsey said a rainy day fund is basic wisdom because something will break or someone will lose a job. He argued that for most average-income households the gap comes from spending, not hard times.
