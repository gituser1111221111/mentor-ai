# Peak-and-valley fund for income swings
source: How to Budget on Commission Income, Heading 'How Much Should You Keep in a Commission Buffer Fund?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-on-commission-income

Rather than paying yourself a fixed salary out of a buffer account, Ramsey Solutions recommends a peak-and-valley fund. Money goes in during strong months and comes out when a month falls short of the budget. Size it from the biggest past monthly deficit. Its purpose is to keep the emergency fund untouched, because a slow month is not an emergency.
