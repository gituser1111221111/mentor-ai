# The credit score measures debt, not wealth
source: Living Without a Credit Score, Heading '2. Your bank account becomes your measuring tool.'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/living-without-credit-score

Ramsey Solutions calls the FICO score a measure of a person's relationship with debt only. It says people can rent, travel and even get a mortgage without a score by paying cash, using debit and saving a large down payment. Success is measured by what is in the bank account.
