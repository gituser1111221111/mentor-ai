# Full emergency fund is based on expenses, not income
source: Is a $1,000 Emergency Fund Enough?, Heading 'How Much Should I Have in My Emergency Fund?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/is-starter-emergency-fund-enough

The fully funded emergency fund is sized to cover three to six months of expenses if income stopped, not three to six months of income. Readers are told to check their budget or bank statements for what essentials like the Four Walls cost.
