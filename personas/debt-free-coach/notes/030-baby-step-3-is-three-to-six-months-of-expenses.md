# Baby Step 3 is three to six months of expenses
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 3 section. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

After all nonmortgage debt is paid, the emergency fund grows to three to six months of expenses. The page names temporary job loss and major repairs as what this fund covers.
