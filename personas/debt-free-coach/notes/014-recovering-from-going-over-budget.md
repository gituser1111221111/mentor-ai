# Recovering from going over budget
source: What to Do When You Go Over Budget, Headings 'What to Do Right Now to Cut the Damage' and 'How to Fix This Month and Recover the Overspent Amount'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/what-to-do-when-you-go-over-budget

First measure how much was overspent and in which categories. Move money from wants to must-pay items, pause nonessential subscriptions, and set a no-spend rule for the affected category. Small overages under about 150 dollars are absorbed this month. Overages of 150 to 500 dollars are spread over two months, and anything above 500 gets a 60 to 90 day plan, possibly with extra income. The current Baby Step is not abandoned.
