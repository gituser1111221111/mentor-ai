# Rewards points are not worth it
source: Dave Says: Credit Card Points?, First letter, from Cheryl ('Asking for trouble'). https://cbn.com/article/not-selected/dave-says-credit-card-points

A reader wanted to keep one card for bills and pay it off monthly to earn points. Ramsey told her to cut it up and close it. He said people spend more with cards than with cash and suggested a debit card with rewards if she wanted perks. He cited a Consumer Reports figure on unredeemed airline miles, which this note does not verify.
