# Debt-free and full emergency fund before investing
source: Ramsey's Investing Philosophy, Heading 'Investing Principle 1: Get out of debt and save up a fully funded emergency fund first.'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/retirement/daves-investing-philosophy

Ramsey Solutions lists getting out of debt and saving three to six months of expenses as the foundation that comes before investing. Only then does the 15 percent retirement contribution begin.
