# No house purchase while still in debt
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, FAQ 'Can I buy my first house when I am paying off debt?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

Buying a home while carrying debt is described as a source of financial stress. The advice is to pay off all debt and build the full emergency fund before taking on a mortgage.
