# Roll over old retirement money, do not cash it out
source: DAVE SAYS (Madill Record), Letter from Todd (forgotten TSP account, Baby Step 1). https://www.madillrecord.net/node/10303

A couple in Baby Step 1 found about 3,200 dollars in an old Thrift Savings Plan and asked about withdrawing it for their Baby Steps. Ramsey said to roll it into an IRA. He compared cashing out, with penalty and taxes he estimated at 30 to 40 percent, to borrowing at that rate.
