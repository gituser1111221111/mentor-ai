# Zero-based budget, with a small cushion
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Heading '3. Subtract your expenses from your income (create a zero-based budget).'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

Income minus planned expenses should equal zero, which means every dollar has an assigned job. This does not mean draining the checking account. The article suggests keeping a cushion of roughly 100 to 300 dollars in the account.
