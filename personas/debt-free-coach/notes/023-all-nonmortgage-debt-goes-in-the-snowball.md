# All nonmortgage debt goes in the snowball
source: How to Get Out of Debt With the Debt Snowball Plan, Heading 'What Debts Should I Include in My Debt Snowball?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/get-out-of-debt-with-the-debt-snowball-plan

The snowball covers every nonmortgage debt, including student loans, medical bills, car loans, credit cards, home equity loans, personal loans and payday loans. The mortgage is handled later in Baby Step 6, after the full emergency fund.
