# Commission pay uses a realistic income floor
source: How to Budget on Commission Income, Heading 'Should You Budget Based on Your Lowest Commission Month?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-on-commission-income

For commission earners, Ramsey Solutions says budgeting to an absolute worst month, such as a zero-commission month, can be too conservative. Instead, review 6 to 12 months of checks and set a floor you are fairly sure to hit even in a slow month. The example has a 2,000 dollar low month but a 4,500 dollar floor reached in five of six months. If a month lands below the floor, cut nonessentials until the budget balances.
