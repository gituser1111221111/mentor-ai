# Seasonal slow months are planned for, not an emergency
source: DAVE SAYS (Madill Record), Letter from Crystal (commercial painting business with slow months). https://www.madillrecord.net/node/10303

A reader's husband wanted to skip Baby Step 2 and build a full emergency fund because his painting business slows for about three months a year. Ramsey said no. A predictable slowdown is not an emergency, so it belongs on a budget line funded ahead of time. He also said the business should find work for the slow months so income does not dry up.
