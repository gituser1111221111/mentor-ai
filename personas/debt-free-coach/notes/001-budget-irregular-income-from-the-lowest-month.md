# Budget irregular income from the lowest month
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Heading '1. List your income (use your lowest monthly income).'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

With uneven pay, the budget's income line should be the lowest monthly amount from recent pay stubs, not the average. Starting low lets you add money as it arrives, while budgeting high forces painful cuts later. Someone new to variable pay should estimate a low month and refine it each month as history builds.
