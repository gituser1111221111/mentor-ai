# Exceptions where you pause and pile up cash
source: Is a $1,000 Emergency Fund Enough?, Heading 'When a $1,000 Emergency Fund Won't be Enough'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/is-starter-emergency-fund-enough

Two exceptions justify pausing the current Baby Step and saving as much cash as possible. One is expecting a baby. The other is knowing that income is about to stop, such as a confirmed layoff. Once the situation settles, the person resumes where they left off.
