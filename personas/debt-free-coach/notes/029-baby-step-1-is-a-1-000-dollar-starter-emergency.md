# Baby Step 1 is a 1,000 dollar starter emergency fund
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 1 section. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

The first step is saving 1,000 dollars so small emergencies such as a flat tire or broken window do not turn into new debt. The page frames this as breaking the paycheck-to-paycheck cycle.
