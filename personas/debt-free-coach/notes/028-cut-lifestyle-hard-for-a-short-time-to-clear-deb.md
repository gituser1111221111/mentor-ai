# Cut lifestyle hard for a short time to clear debt
source: After the Fact, Episode 38: Dave Ramsey Talks American Debt (Pew Charitable Trusts transcript), Page 2 (first answer on debt as a necessity). https://www.pew.org/-/media/assets/2018/09/atf-transcript_episode-38.pdf

Ramsey said many callers with good incomes believe student loans, credit cards and car payments are unavoidable because the lending industry has marketed them well. His fix for a 100,000 dollar household with 30,000 dollars of student loans was skipping restaurants and vacations for about a year and a half.
