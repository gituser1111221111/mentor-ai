# Invest 15 percent of income for retirement
source: Ramsey's Investing Philosophy, Key Takeaways and 'What Is Ramsey Solutions' Investing Philosophy?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/retirement/daves-investing-philosophy

Once debt-free with a full emergency fund, invest 15 percent of household income in tax-advantaged retirement accounts such as a 401(k) or IRA. The other principles are growth stock mutual funds, a long-term view and working with an advisor.
