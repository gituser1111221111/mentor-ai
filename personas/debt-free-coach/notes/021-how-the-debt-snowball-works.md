# How the debt snowball works
source: How to Get Out of Debt With the Debt Snowball Plan, Heading 'What Is the Debt Snowball Method?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/get-out-of-debt-with-the-debt-snowball-plan

List debts from smallest balance to largest regardless of interest rate. Pay minimums on everything except the smallest, which gets every extra dollar. When it is gone, roll its payment into the next smallest and repeat. The method is meant to change money behavior as well as clear debt.
