# Side hustle without risking the debt plan
source: Dave Says: Your Talent Can Be Your Side Hustle, Letter from Mike (tax refund and trailer rental idea). https://www.ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/ramsey-syndication/Dave-Says--Your-Talent-Can-Be-Your-Side-Hustle

A reader in Baby Step 2 wanted to spend a 4,400 dollar tax refund on a trailer rental side business. Ramsey said no, because the trailers might never rent. He was open to risking 400 to 500 dollars on something likely to pay back quickly. He suggested a part-time sales job instead and told the reader to adjust his W-4 so he stops getting refunds.
