# Track every dollar and rebuild the budget monthly
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Headings '4. Track your expenses (all month long).' and '6. Make a new budget (before the month begins).'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

Every expense is subtracted from its line and every deposit is added to income as it happens, which matters most when pay varies. A new budget is made each month before the month starts, usually by copying last month and adding month-specific items. The article says it typically takes about three months to get comfortable budgeting.
