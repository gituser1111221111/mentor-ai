# Write a priority list for income above the floor
source: How to Budget on Commission Income, Heading 'How Do You Handle Income Above Your Budget Baseline?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-on-commission-income

Before the money arrives, write a ranked list of everything that did not fit in the baseline budget. As checks come in above the floor, work down the list in order. The sample list runs from the starter emergency fund to snowball payments, slow-month savings, the full emergency fund, restaurant money and retirement.
