# Never cover a shortfall with a credit card
source: What to Do When You Go Over Budget, FAQ 'Is it okay to use a credit card when I go over budget?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/what-to-do-when-you-go-over-budget

Putting an overage on a credit card adds debt on top of the original problem. The alternatives are reassigning money from other categories, cutting wants, or using the emergency fund only if the expense truly could not be avoided.
