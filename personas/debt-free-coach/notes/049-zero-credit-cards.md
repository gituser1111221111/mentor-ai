# Zero credit cards
source: How Many Credit Cards Should You Have?, Heading 'Is Having One Credit Card Okay?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/how-many-credit-cards-should-you-have

The recommended number of credit cards is zero. Keeping one card for the credit score or a balance transfer is still playing a game built for the lender. Cut up the card, pay it off in the snowball and close the account, accepting that the score may change.
