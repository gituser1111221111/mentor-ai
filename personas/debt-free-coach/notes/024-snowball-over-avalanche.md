# Snowball over avalanche
source: Debt Avalanche vs. Debt Snowball: What's the Difference?, Heading 'Debt Snowball vs. Debt Avalanche'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/debt-snowball-vs-debt-avalanche

The avalanche orders debts from highest to lowest rate and can save interest. Ramsey Solutions says it may mean paying a large balance first and waiting a long time for a first win, which makes quitting more likely. The snowball is presented as the method people actually finish.
