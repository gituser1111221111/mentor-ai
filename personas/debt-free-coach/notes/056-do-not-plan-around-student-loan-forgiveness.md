# Do not plan around student loan forgiveness
source: Should I Apply for Student Loan Forgiveness?, Key Takeaways. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/student-loan-forgiveness

Forgiveness programs require years of qualifying payments and specific jobs, and Public Service Loan Forgiveness approves very few applicants. Ramsey Solutions recommends a budget and the debt snowball instead of waiting for forgiveness.
