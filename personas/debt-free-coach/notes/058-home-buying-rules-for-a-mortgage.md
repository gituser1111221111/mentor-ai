# Home-buying rules for a mortgage
source: What Is a 15-Year Fixed-Rate Mortgage?, Opening section on recommended mortgage terms. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/real-estate/what-is-a-15-year-fixed-rate-mortgage

Paying cash is best. If borrowing, Ramsey Solutions recommends a 15-year fixed-rate conventional mortgage with at least 10 percent down, ideally 20 percent to avoid PMI. The monthly payment should stay at or below 25 percent of take-home pay.
