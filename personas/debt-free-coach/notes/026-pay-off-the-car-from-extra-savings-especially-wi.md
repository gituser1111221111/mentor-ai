# Pay off the car from extra savings, especially with variable pay
source: Dave Says: Debt is Like a Ball and Chain, Letter from Mike (sales income, 10,000 dollar car loan, large savings). https://www.ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/ramsey-syndication/Dave-Says--Debt-is-Like-a-Ball-and-Chain

A salesman with fluctuating pay had a six-month emergency fund plus 25,000 dollars in other savings and a 10,000 dollar car loan at 600 dollars a month. He asked whether to keep the cash because his income varies. Ramsey said to pay off the car from savings that day. He argued that debt is the last thing someone with unpredictable pay should carry and that the freed 600 dollars would rebuild savings quickly.
