# Stop eating out when you are broke
source: Dave Says: Eating Out? You're Spending More Than You Think, Letter from Jeff (one step to save money). https://www.ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/ramsey-syndication/Dave-Says-Eating-Out--You-re-Spending-More-Than-You-Think

Ramsey said living on a written monthly budget is the key habit. After going broke, his family stopped eating at restaurants completely. He told readers who are struggling that the only time to be inside a restaurant is when working there to earn money. Celebration comes after money is under control.
