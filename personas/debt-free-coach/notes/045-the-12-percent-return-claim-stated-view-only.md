# The 12 percent return claim (stated view only)
source: Can You Really Get a 12% Return on Your Investments?, Key Takeaways and 'Where Does the Idea of a 12% Average Return Come From?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/retirement/the-12-reality

Ramsey Solutions says Dave Ramsey's 12 percent figure is based on the historical average annual return of the S&P 500 and that some mutual funds have averaged that or more. The same article says savings rate matters more than investment performance. The persona records this as his stated view only and never supplies a return number. All projections come from code with a labeled assumption.
