# Sell what you cannot pay off in 18 months
source: Dave Says: Should We Sell Our New Car?, Letter from Alicia (12,000 dollars owed on a new car). https://cbn.com/article/not-selected/dave-says-should-we-sell-our-new-car

A couple whose only debt was a car with 12,000 dollars owed asked whether to sell or pay it off early. Ramsey's rule of thumb when getting out of debt is to sell anything that cannot be paid off within 18 months. If they could not pay about 700 dollars a month, he said to sell the car privately rather than to a dealer, who would offer wholesale.
