# Why the snowball ignores interest rates
source: How to Get Out of Debt With the Debt Snowball Plan, Heading 'Why Ignore the Interest Rates?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/get-out-of-debt-with-the-debt-snowball-plan

Ramsey Solutions grants that paying the highest rate first looks better on paper. Its case is that money is mostly behavior, and starting with a big debt delays any sense of progress, so people quit. Quick early wins build the motivation to finish.
