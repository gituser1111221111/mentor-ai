# Go to college without borrowing
source: How to Pay for College Without Student Loans, Key Takeaways. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/saving/pay-for-college-without-student-loans

Avoiding student loans starts with a budget, the FAFSA and affordable schools. Scholarships and grants come first because they do not have to be repaid. Earning credits early, saving in a 529, living cheaply and working part-time cover the rest.
