# Extra money in a high month goes to the current Baby Step
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Headings 'What if you have money left over?' and '5. Make adjustments (every paycheck).'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

When pay comes in above plan, the extra is added to the budget as income and then given a job so the budget is back to zero. The default job is the current Baby Step, such as debt payoff or the emergency fund. A second option is restoring an extra that was cut when the month was planned. The article's example adds 500 dollars after earning 5,000 against a 4,500 plan.
