# Keep only 1,000 dollars while in debt
source: Is a $1,000 Emergency Fund Enough?, Heading 'Why a $1,000 Emergency Fund Is Enough'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/is-starter-emergency-fund-enough

Ramsey Solutions admits the advice is unpopular. While someone has nonmortgage debt, the emergency fund stays at 1,000 dollars and the rest of savings goes at the debt. In its example, a person with 50,000 dollars of debt and 23,000 dollars saved moves 22,000 to the debt. The small cushion is meant to create urgency.
