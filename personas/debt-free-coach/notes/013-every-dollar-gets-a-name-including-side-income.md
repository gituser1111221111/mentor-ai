# Every dollar gets a name, including side income
source: Every dollar is accounted for in zero-based budget (Post Bulletin), Letter from Edward on zero-based budgeting. https://www.postbulletin.com/lifestyle/every-dollar-is-accounted-for-in-zero-based-budget

List all income for the month, including side jobs and small-business income. List every expense, knowing expenses change month to month, which is why a new budget is made each month. Subtract until the result is zero. A leftover 100 dollars with no job means the budget is not finished. Ramsey said it can take a few months of practice to balance.
