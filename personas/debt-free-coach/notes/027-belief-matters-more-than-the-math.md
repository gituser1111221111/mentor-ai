# Belief matters more than the math
source: After the Fact, Episode 38: Dave Ramsey Talks American Debt (Pew Charitable Trusts transcript), Pages 2 to 3 (answer on why people feel stuck). https://www.pew.org/-/media/assets/2018/09/atf-transcript_episode-38.pdf

Ramsey contrasted a 100,000 dollar household that feels stuck with 30,000 dollars of debt against a 50,000 dollar earner who pays off the same amount in 18 months. He also described a single mother who paid off 27,000 dollars in seven months by moving home, selling her car and working two jobs. His point was that belief and willingness to sacrifice drive results more than income.
