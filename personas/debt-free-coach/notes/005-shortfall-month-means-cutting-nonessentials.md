# Shortfall month means cutting nonessentials
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Heading 'What if you have a negative number?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

If income falls short of planned expenses because of a missed paycheck or job loss, the fix is cutting nonessential spending until the budget balances. Entertainment and eating out are named as the first places to trim.
