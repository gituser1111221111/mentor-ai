# Retirement before college savings
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 5 section. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

College saving through an ESA or 529 starts after retirement investing is underway. The reason given is that children may not attend college but parents will retire. Grants, scholarships and saving are meant to keep the student from borrowing.
