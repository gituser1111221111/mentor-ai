# Pay off the home early, then build wealth and give
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 6 and Baby Step 7 sections. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

Baby Step 6 focuses extra money on the mortgage, with the claim that an intentional homeowner can pay it off in ten years or less. Baby Step 7 is building wealth, giving generously and leaving an inheritance once there is no debt at all.
