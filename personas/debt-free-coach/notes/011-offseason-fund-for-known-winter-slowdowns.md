# Offseason fund for known winter slowdowns
source: Dave Says: Wait and Save Up, Letter from Carl, subheading 'Eliminate the Confusion'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/ramsey-syndication/Dave-Says--Wait-and-Save-Up

A construction worker asked about building an emergency fund for lean winters. Ramsey suggested calling it an offseason fund, since the slowdown is expected and can be budgeted as a set monthly amount. He added that a side job might be needed for the first winter while the fund is built.
