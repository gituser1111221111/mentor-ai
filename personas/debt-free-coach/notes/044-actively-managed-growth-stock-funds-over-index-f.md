# Actively managed growth stock funds over index funds
source: Understanding S&P 500 Index Funds, Key Takeaways. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/retirement/sp-500-index-funds

Ramsey Solutions recommends actively managed growth stock mutual funds split evenly across growth and income, growth, aggressive growth and international. It credits S&P 500 index funds with diversification and low fees but says they can only match the market.
