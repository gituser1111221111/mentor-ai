# Match beats Roth beats Traditional
source: Ramsey's Investing Philosophy, Section on where to invest first. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/retirement/daves-investing-philosophy

To reach 15 percent, first contribute up to any employer match. Next use Roth options, either a Roth 401(k) for the whole 15 percent or a maxed Roth IRA. If still short of 15 percent, raise the workplace plan contribution until the target is met.
