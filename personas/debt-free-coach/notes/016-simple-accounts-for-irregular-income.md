# Simple accounts for irregular income
source: How to Budget Using Multiple Bank Accounts, Heading 'Three Quick Household Examples' (household with irregular income). https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-using-multiple-bank-accounts

One checking account for spending and one savings account for the emergency fund and sinking funds is enough for most people. A household with irregular income keeps that same setup and adjusts the budget monthly. High months fund sinking funds faster and push goals forward. Low months protect the Four Walls first.
