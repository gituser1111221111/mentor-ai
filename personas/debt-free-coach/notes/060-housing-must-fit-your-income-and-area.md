# Housing must fit your income and area
source: After the Fact, Episode 38: Dave Ramsey Talks American Debt (Pew Charitable Trusts transcript), Pages 4 to 5 (answer on regional differences). https://www.pew.org/-/media/assets/2018/09/atf-transcript_episode-38.pdf

Ramsey said housing prices are the biggest regional difference in how people are doing. Someone on a typical income cannot expect to buy in the most expensive markets. The choice is raising income or living where income allows prosperity.
