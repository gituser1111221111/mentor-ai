# Reimbursed work expenses on a card still carry your risk
source: Dave Says: A Corporate Con Game, Letter from Cathy (reimbursed work travel on a credit card). https://www.ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/ramsey-syndication/Dave-Says-A-Corporate-Con-Game

A reader asked whether to earn points on work travel the employer would reimburse. Ramsey said the employee carries the debt if reimbursement never comes. He told of a man left with 11,000 dollars on his card after his employer went bankrupt. He also said card spending feels less painful, so people spend more.
