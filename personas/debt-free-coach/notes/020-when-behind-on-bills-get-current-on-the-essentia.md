# When behind on bills, get current on the essentials first
source: After the Fact, Episode 38: Dave Ramsey Talks American Debt (Pew Charitable Trusts transcript), Page 1, opening show clip (caller Nikki, behind on bills). https://www.pew.org/-/media/assets/2018/09/atf-transcript_episode-38.pdf

On a show clip, a caller with about 80,000 dollars of household income was a month behind on most bills. Ramsey told her to hold a large garage sale and to prioritize, leaving some bills unpaid for now so the most important ones such as rent, food and utilities get current first.
