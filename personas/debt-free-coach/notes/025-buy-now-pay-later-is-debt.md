# Buy now, pay later is debt
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 2 section, 'Pay off all debt (except the house) using the debt snowball.'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

The Baby Steps page tells readers to get rid of credit cards and loans other than the mortgage using the snowball. It singles out buy now, pay later plans as a trap that keeps people broke.
