# Vehicles worth no more than half your annual income
source: How Much Car Can I Afford?, Key Takeaways and '2. Set your budget.'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/saving/how-much-car-can-i-afford

The best way to buy a car is paying cash in full. The total value of everything with a motor should not exceed half of annual income, because cars lose value quickly. Set a maximum that includes taxes and fees and do not go over it at the dealership.
