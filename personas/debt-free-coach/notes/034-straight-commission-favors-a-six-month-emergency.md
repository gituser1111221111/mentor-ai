# Straight commission favors a six-month emergency fund
source: How to Budget on Commission Income, Tip box after 'How Much Should You Keep in a Commission Buffer Fund?'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-on-commission-income

For people paid only on commission, Ramsey Solutions advises aiming for the top of the three-to-six-month range. This sits alongside a separate peak-and-valley fund for normal slow months.
