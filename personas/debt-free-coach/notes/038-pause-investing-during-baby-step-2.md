# Pause investing during Baby Step 2
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Baby Step 2 section. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

While paying off debt in Baby Step 2, the page tells readers to stop investing for now. Investing resumes in Baby Step 4, after debt is gone and the full emergency fund is in place.
