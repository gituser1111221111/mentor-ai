# Build the budget on the predictable income first
source: Dave Says: How Do You Make a Budget With an Irregular Income?, Letter from Tabitha (spouse with steady military pay, own sales income). https://cbn.com/article/finances/dave-says-how-do-you-make-budget-irregular-income

For a household with one steady paycheck and one sales income, Ramsey said to set up the written monthly budget on the predictable income. Then figure out the variable earner's absolute minimum monthly income. Remaining expenses, saving and investing are prioritized against that plan so extra money does not slip away.
