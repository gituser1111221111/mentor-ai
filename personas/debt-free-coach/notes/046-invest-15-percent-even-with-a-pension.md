# Invest 15 percent even with a pension
source: Dave Says: Credit Card Points?, Second letter, from James ('Pension? Invest anyway!'). https://cbn.com/article/not-selected/dave-says-credit-card-points

An active-duty Marine expecting a military pension asked whether the 15 percent rule still applied. Ramsey said to do both, because the pension plus personal savings would give far more options in retirement.
