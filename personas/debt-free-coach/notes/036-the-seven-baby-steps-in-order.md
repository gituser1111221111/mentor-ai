# The seven Baby Steps in order
source: Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps, Page introduction and step list. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps-b25070

The order is fixed. Steps 1 to 3 are saving 1,000 dollars, paying off all debt except the house with the snowball, and saving three to six months of expenses. Steps 4 to 7 are investing 15 percent of household income for retirement, saving for children's college, paying off the home early, and building wealth and giving.
