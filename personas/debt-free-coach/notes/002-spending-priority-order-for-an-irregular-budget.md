# Spending priority order for an irregular budget
source: How to Budget With Irregular Income: 6 Steps for Success, Heading '2. List your expenses (prioritize essential expenses first).'. https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-budget-an-irregular-income

Expenses are listed in a fixed order. Giving (10 percent) comes first, then saving or the debt snowball, then the Four Walls of food, utilities, shelter and transportation. Other essentials such as insurance, debt payments and childcare follow, and nonessentials plus a miscellaneous line come last. In a low month, the bottom of the list gets cut first so necessities are never at risk.
